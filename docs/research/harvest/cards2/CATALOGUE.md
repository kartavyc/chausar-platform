How to read a row: **Sources** name the product and the file in its repo. **Licence**: reusable (keep notices) or learn-only. **Value** is for a B2C owner (H, M, L). **Effort** S, M, L. **Wave**: W1 safety and bug fixes; W2 wire built-but-unreachable code; W3 Ink v2 look and places; W4 new features; W5 new and deeper playbooks; "Later" means planned but not in this build. **Already in Chausar?** cites `build-audit-2026-10-23.md` ("audit") or code in chausar-platform; "ink.md" is `docs/venture/ink.md`, "design" is `docs/superpowers/designs/2026-10-21-chausar-frontend-ink.md`.

Model-written numbers are allowed only labelled "model estimate" (decision of 23 Oct); every row below that mentions a score or figure follows that rule.
