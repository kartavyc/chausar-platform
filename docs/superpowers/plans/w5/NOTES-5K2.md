Status: in progress, not reviewed or complete.

Read the package, BK3/BK5/BK6/K15 catalogue rows and finance2 cards for the MIT marketplace-to-books, Lexware and finance workspace patterns. Existing accounting read adapters, seller settlement engine, site-edit journal flow and bookkeeping desk were inspected.

Official connector sources checked on 28 October 2026:
- https://www.zoho.com/developer/oauth/self-client/overview.html and authorization-code-flow.html: an owner self client is for the owner's own account; the documented flow does not require marketplace review. Client-owned books require that client's authorized credentials, not an agency-wide assumed grant.
- https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/invoices/: invoice creation defaults to draft; marking sent is a separate endpoint. Email and other delivery actions are not implied by creating a draft.
- https://help.tallysolutions.com/xml-integration/ and https://help.tallysolutions.com/pre-requisites-for-integrations/: Tally supports an explicitly enabled local XML HTTP server. The intended connector is desktop-only and pinned to the owner's local endpoint and selected company.

Initial implementation: browser-safe `posting-core.ts`, five tests in `venture-accounting-write.test.ts`. Missing-module RED recorded before implementation; all five pass. Ordered decisions retain Indian tax amounts, fit line totals in integer paise, stop a batch on failed or ambiguous existing-record reads, derive dates in the shop zone and give reversals their own external ID.

Conservative decision: changed existing accounting records are held for manual review, not silently rewritten. Partial refund amounts and cancelled unissued drafts are also held. A matching total does not grant authority to issue or send. Actual adapters, journal posting, cycles, setup, vouchers, seller reconciliation integration, tools, desk, fake parity, Hindi, baselines and independent review remain to build.
