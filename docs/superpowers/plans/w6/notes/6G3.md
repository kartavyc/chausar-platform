# 6G3 CRM upgrades (D44)

## What was built

- **Field history with who and when** (`platform/runtime/venture/crm/`). One `venture-crm-change` row per changed field (subject, field, before, after, who, when, ref), written beside the record. The client record is never rewritten to hold its history. A client kept before wave 6 has no rows and reads as "Added to Clients" (from its own `at`); its id and shape are unchanged (the test checks the record's keys before and after an edit).
  - A client's name, email and note change through the evidence ledger, as before. `venture.crm.client.edit` keeps the owner's value as owner-entered evidence, and the ledger's write-back (`evidence/ledger.ts writeThrough`) records the row. So an agent's checked value that applies itself is in the same history as "An agent", and a suggestion the owner applies is "You".
  - References (a client's company, a company's members) store ids. The view names them at read time, so a rename shows everywhere and Forget has fewer copies to find.
- **Pipelines as lists** (`venture.crm.lists`, `.list`, `.list.save`, `.list.place`). The owner's own lists (up to 30), each with two to eight stages of its own. A client, person or company sits in a list at one stage (up to 500 entries a list). Every move is a change row on that member ("You changed Workshops: Asked (was To ask)"). A stage that still holds entries can't be dropped. The main pipeline keeps its board and its forward-only rules. Nothing in a list is ever sent.
- **A Company record** (`venture-company`, `venture-crm-link`): name (named once, case and spaces folded), website (`https://www.Aroma.example/x` becomes `aroma.example`), note. One link record per member gives two-way links: the client view shows its company, and the company view lists its clients and people. Company edits and member changes are in the company's history. `venture.crm.link` with `companyId: null` takes a member out. The same link again changes nothing.
- **Timeline entries** (`venture.crm.client`, `venture.crm.log`): newest first, at most 200.
  - What the owner logged: a call, a WhatsApp message, a meeting or a note, with the day (never after today) and their words.
  - The sends and replies recorded on the client's pipeline cards (a Won card that made the client, or a card of a person linked to them), with the channel in words ("You called", "You sent a WhatsApp message").
  - The client's invoices, issued, part paid, paid or cancelled (drafts left out). No amount is shown on the timeline: the invoice list carries the receipted figure.
  - The company sheet merges its clients' timelines.
- **Forget** (`network/forget.ts` calls `eraseCrmNaming`). The change rows about the person and their client go, and so do rows naming either as a member, rows whose values name them or hold their email or phone, their company links, their list places and the entries logged for their client. What stays loses their name: another client's entry reads "Erased: it named someone you asked Chausar to forget.", a company note is cleared, a company or list name holding theirs reads "A company" or "A list". An import's Undo takes the links and list places of the people it removes. The client record stays, as Forget already did.
- **UI** (Clients place; no reference drawing exists, so it extends the current client sheet):
  - The client sheet gains Company (opens the new company sheet, or "Put in a company": an existing one or a new one), Timeline with "Log a call or message" folded (it says Chausar has no WhatsApp connection), Lists with "Put in a list", What Chausar remembers (6G4), and Changes and Change details folded.
  - New sheets `company:<id>`, `lists:all` and `list:<id>` (`CompanySheet.tsx`, `ListsSheet.tsx`, `ClientCrm.tsx`, `CrmParts.tsx`, `Crm.module.css`, text in `text/en/crm.json`). The Pipeline tab's tools gain a "Lists" button.
- **Fake engine** (`engine/fake-crm.ts`): uses the engine's own pure file (`crm/crm-data.ts`) for every check, view and word. A client edit writes the fake's evidence the way the engine's ledger does. The fake's evidence apply writes a history row too. Pipeline touches come from the fake pipeline desk (`clientTouches`), and Forget follows `forgetPeople`.

New methods (11): `venture.crm.client`, `venture.crm.client.edit`, `venture.crm.companies`, `venture.crm.company`, `venture.crm.company.save`, `venture.crm.link`, `venture.crm.log`, `venture.crm.lists`, `venture.crm.list`, `venture.crm.list.save`, `venture.crm.list.place`. Owner `network`. The writes are app-only, level prepare, local only (nothing reaches outside).

## Deviations and why

- **"Pipelines as lists" read as owner lists with their own stages.** The main pipeline's forward-only, evidence-backed stages stay on the board unchanged. The founder may also have meant a list view of the main board. That is a small UI-only follow-up on `venture.pipeline.list`, left out to keep the Pipeline tab's word budget.
- **WhatsApp messages and calls are logged by the owner.** Chausar has no WhatsApp connection (labelled honestly in the log form). Recorded sends on cards with channel `whatsapp` or `phone` also reach the timeline.
- **The person sheet does not yet show its company.** The engine links people (tested, and the company sheet lists them), but a person-side read and control would need one more method. Left as an open item.
- **Timeline invoice entries carry no amount.** It avoids a second receipted sum path; the invoice list has the figure with its receipt.
