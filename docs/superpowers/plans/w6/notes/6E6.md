# 6E6: The 57-profession map (D1, D2, D21)

## What was built

- **One data file**, `platform/runtime/venture/core/professions.ts` (pure, so the fake engine reads it too). For each of the 57 professions it holds:
  - the id and label (the same ids as 6C3's price data);
  - the family (families.ts ids);
  - two or three offer names;
  - the main `playbook` and the others it can use (`also`);
  - its `cautions` (statutes) and `hardStops` (policy/hard-stops.ts ids);
  - its `size`: the usual shape of one client job, `task`, `monthly` or `project`. No figure is involved.
  - Helpers: `professionOf` (also reads the old 6C6 ids `architect`, `home-chef` and `call-centre-agent`), `professionsForPlaybook`, `familiesForPlaybook`, `cautionOf` and `validateProfessions`.
- **Playbooks** follow the PACKAGES table:
  - teaching on `lesson-desk`;
  - local services on `booking-desk`;
  - documents on `document-desk` (tender writers on `tender-desk`, with `document-desk` also);
  - licensed work and the company secretary on `signoff-desk`;
  - `trading-journal` as an extra for the CA and the retired tax staff (traders' tax packs);
  - every other profession on its existing playbook.
- **No drifting lists.** `validatePriceRanges` now fails unless the price data names the same 57 ids, labels, order and families as the map, and every `WAY_PRICES` way's profession is one the map runs on that way. The compliance check (`core/compliance-data.ts`) is now built from the map: every profession answers, with its statutes, hard stops and (for advocates) switch-offs.
- **The nine statutes**, all read on 1 November 2026. Each has an id, title, line, instrument, section and sources. A statute counts as checked only if its text was opened on a gov.in or nic.in host.

  | Statute | Status | Source read |
  |---|---|---|
  | RTE Act section 28 | checked | megscpcr.gov.in copy of the Act |
  | BCI Rule 36 | unchecked | site draws rules with JavaScript; India Code refused |
  | Telemedicine Guidelines 2020 | unchecked | mohfw.gov.in refused; the nhm.gov.in copy is a scan |
  | SEBI IA 2013 and RA 2014, reg 3(1) | checked | sebi.gov.in (original texts; later amendments not read) |
  | Architects Act s.37(1) | checked | quoted in full in a Supreme Court judgment on api.sci.gov.in |
  | RERA 2016 ss.9 and 10 | checked | reraonline.kerala.gov.in copy of the Act |
  | FSS Act 2006 s.31 and 2011 regulations | checked | fssai.gov.in |
  | NCAHP Act 2021 s.15 and Schedule | checked, corrected | Gazette copy on thc.nic.in. The old line ("allied health law of their state") was wrong: it is a central Act with State and Central Registers. |
  | TCCCPR 2018 reg 3(2), 2025 amendment | checked | trai.gov.in. A September 2026 TRAI regulation was not read. |

  - I ran the research through a helper agent and tried BCI again myself. The extracted texts are in my scratchpad, not the repo.
  - No section number came from anywhere but the official text read.
- **Compliance screen** (`ComplianceCheck.tsx`): a checked statute links its official page as "Official text, checked 1 Nov 2026". The engine sends `source`, `checkedOn` and `checkedDay`, so the UI formats nothing. Unchecked ones keep "Not checked against the official page yet".
- **The gallery shows the families.**
  - Engine: each `WayView` carries `families` and `WaysView.families` lists the families that have a way, with counts (`core/ways.ts`).
  - UI: a "Family" list beside Sort, in the same style (`GalleryFilters.tsx`, `ways.ts`, `index.tsx`). It replaces the four category chips, because the onboarding word budget only shrinks: this saves about 9 words. A family and the hours chip narrow together.
  - The fake answers from `fixtures-start.json`, which was already regenerated with families and is pinned by venture-ways.

## Deviations

- "Size" is not defined in PACKAGES. I read it as the shape of a client job (task, monthly, project), not a market size, because a market size would need sourced figures. Please confirm or redefine.
- Multi-profession desks (lesson, booking, document, sign-off) have no `WAY_PRICES` row. That table names one profession per way, and showing one profession's guide (for example an advocate's) on a desk that serves several would mislead. The plan should take the guide from the owner's profession, through `priceGuide(profession)` and `professionOf`.
- The category chips were replaced by the Family list, not kept beside it, because of the word budget.
- The expected-pending desk list was removed from the test, because all five desks are now registered.
