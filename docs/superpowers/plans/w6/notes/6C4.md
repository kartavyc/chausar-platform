# 6C4: Two launch stages (D19)

## What was built

- The owner sees two stages: **Practice** and **Live (you approve each)**. `venture.launch.status` now returns `{ stage: 'practice' | 'live', stages: [practice, live] with allowed, reason and current, acceptedDeliveries, improvement }`. The unfinished stages (approve a sample, auto with spot checks) are never listed.
- Shadow is folded into Practice. The vault keeps its old stage names so existing businesses still load: `rehearsal` and `shadow` read as Practice, `approve-each` reads as Live (`platform/runtime/venture/core/launch-stage.ts`, pure, shared with the fake). In Practice a real order now gets an AI draft marked as a practice draft that can never be accepted or reach a client (previously the `rehearsal` stage refused AI drafts outright with LAUNCH_STAGE_BLOCKED).
- `venture.launch.advance { to: 'practice' | 'live' }` (approve, app only). Up to Live needs an accepted plan and a connected AI (the same check `venture.delivery.run` uses to choose an AI draft over a template outline). Down to Practice is always allowed and needs no AI. `auto-spot-check` still answers AUTO_LOCKED; every other old name answers INVALID_VENTURE_REQUEST.
- Retired gate removed: the three judged made-up-client rehearsals that the old per-turn loop required before real orders. Rehearsals stay as a Practice feature on the same page.
- Business > Practice gets a "Stage" tile (`app/ui/src/places/business/LaunchStages.tsx`): the two stages, the engine's line, and one button. "Go live" asks a second time ("Go live now"), then calls `venture.launch.advance`; "Back to practice" needs no second ask. A blocked Live keeps the button locked (aria-disabled) with the engine's reason under it.
- Fake engine: `app/ui/src/engine/fake-launch.ts` answers both methods with the engine's words; `venture.get` now returns the stored stage; fake AI jobs for real orders are practice in Practice.
