# 6G1 Chat states (D42)

## What was built

Engine (`platform/runtime/venture/chief/`):
- `states.ts` (new): every Chats thread is Pinned, Active, Snoozed or Settled.
  - Pinned, snoozed (its wake time) and settled-by-the-owner are stored on the thread (`snoozedUntil`, `settledAt`, `stateAt`, all optional, so old vaults read as before).
  - Auto-settle is worked out on every read. A chat settles once it has been quiet for the owner's number of days (7 by default, 0 is never). Quiet is counted from the latest of its last message, the owner's last state change and its last snooze wake. Nothing runs in the background and nothing is written for it.
  - A chat that waits for the owner is never hidden. Waiting means a drafted or raised step in it, or the chief's question back ("Which agent...?") is still the last word. Such a chat does not settle, wakes from a snooze early, and the owner cannot snooze or settle it until it stops waiting.
  - Snooze presets are worked out by the engine on the owner's clock in the business's time zone: in 3 hours, tomorrow 09:00, next Monday 09:00. On a Sunday, tomorrow and next Monday are the same minute, so only one is offered.
  - `venture.chief.thread.state { threadId, state, preset?, until? }` (prepare, app only) returns `{ thread, undo, line }`. `undo` is the exact request that restores the previous state, including the same wake time.
  - `venture.chief.settle.set { days }` (prepare, app only) takes 0 to 365 and returns `{ days, undo, line }`. The choice is stored as a new `venture-chat-settle` record; the newest one is in use.
  - Both go through `ventureAction`, so the activity log records `venture.chief.state` and `venture.chief.settle` against the member.
  - No message, proposal or receipt is touched.
- `threads.ts`:
  - The summary now carries `state`, `snoozedUntil`, `settled {at, by}` and `waiting`.
  - The list has a `snoozed` group at the end. Settled chats leave the groups and go to `settled` (the archive, latest first), with `settleAfterDays`.
  - `thread.list` carries `snooze` (empty while waiting).
  - The owner writing in a snoozed or settled chat brings it back. The chief's `ask` is now stored on its message.
  - `venture.chief.thread.pin` is kept and behaves as state pinned or active.
- Registered in contracts (kinds, record union, types, methods, owners, app-only, params, results), `records.ts` prefix, `engine.ts`, `method-hints.ts`, the desktop IPC allowlist and its `.d.cts`, and the UI `types.ts`.

App:
- A chat's head has a "Chat actions" group:
  - Pin.
  - Snooze, which shows the engine's presets with wake times. When the chat is snoozed, this button is Wake now.
  - Settle, or Reopen when the chat is settled.
  - On a waiting chat, Snooze and Settle are `aria-disabled`. Clicking one says why in plain words, and the app sends nothing.
- Every change shows the engine's line with Undo (`ChangeNotice.tsx`). Undo sends the engine's `undo` request.
- Chats list:
  - The Snoozed group comes last and shows when each chat comes back.
  - Settled chats are not listed.
  - A quiet history-icon link beside "Your chats" opens Settled chats. Its accessible name is "N settled chats", and it adds no visible words.
- Settled chats (`#/business/chats`, `SettledChats.tsx`), linked from the business menu as "Settled chats". It is listed among the business pages only while it is the page, like Your rules. It has:
  - the quiet-days choice (After 3, 7, 14 or 30 quiet days, or Never, plus the owner's own number if it is a different one), with Undo;
  - each settled chat with why it settled ("You settled it on 23 Oct" or "Settled on 18 Oct after 7 quiet days"), a link to open it, and Reopen with Undo.
- Rail: "New" no longer shows when every chat is settled.
- Fake engine (`fake-chief.ts`): same states, groups, archive, presets (Kolkata), words, refusals and Undo.
  - The sample trailer chat moved from 9 to 6 days ago, so it is still listed.
  - A new sample chat from 12 days ago has settled on its own, so the archive has something in it.

## Deviations and choices
- Scope is the Chats threads (chief of staff). An agent's own chat in Team is one thread per agent; 6G2's Retired shelf covers it.
- Auto-settle is worked out on each read instead of by a background sweep, as T3 Code does it. It is deterministic, and the fake answers the same. The owner's quiet days are a setting (D29) with 7 as the default.
- Undo of an auto-settle reopen puts the chat back as settled by the owner, not by the quiet days. Every other Undo restores exactly.
- The reference UI has no drawn chat-states screen (Main.dc.html is "Connect your AI"). The controls follow the current Chats head: quiet small buttons, no new colours.
- T3 Code (MIT, T3 Tools Inc.) was read for its rules only. Nothing was copied.
