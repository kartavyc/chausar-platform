# 6C3 Price ranges with sources (D17)

## What was built

- **Data file** `platform/runtime/venture/core/price-ranges-data.ts`. It holds all 57 beta professions in the 11 families of PACKAGES 6E6. Each sourced range carries its currency (INR, or USD where the source states dollars), BigInt minor amounts, a kind and a unit ("per hour", "per return"...), the city where the source is local, its sources (https link, publisher, title, the source's own date when it shows one), the date checked (1 Nov 2026) and a plain note on how the range was read. Kind is `range`; `minimums` for ICAI's "and above" scale, which reads "... or more"; or `cap` for SEBI's legal maximum, which reads "Up to ...". A profession with no credible source has no range and a one-line reason, and it shows "No reliable data".
- **Code** `platform/runtime/venture/core/price-ranges.ts`. `priceGuide(profession)` builds the view, and code writes every amount's words from minor units. `wayPrice(playbook)` is the gallery's pay line. `priceGuideForPlaybook(playbook, country)` gives the plan its guide, India only. `validatePriceRanges()` checks the data, and `WAY_PRICES` maps each way to its profession and, only where the service and unit match, the range it shows. The fake engine imports this file directly, so the fake and the engine answer alike. The view types live there and are re-exported from `venture/contracts.ts`, which keeps the UI typecheck from pulling in the whole contracts graph.
- **Wiring**
  - Ways gallery (`core/ways.ts`): where a credible range prices the way's own service in the same unit, it replaces the model estimate. `clientsPay.estimate` is false, `source` names the publishers and the checked date, and `rangeId` says which range. Every mapped way also carries `clientsPay.guide`.
  - Today that rule replaces one tile: Bookkeeping and CFO help shows ₹499 to ₹15,000 per month (IndiaMART). Every other way keeps its labelled model estimate, with the profession's guide (sources, or "No reliable data") in the tile's fold.
  - The plan view (`blueprintView` in `core/lookups.ts`) carries `priceGuide` for the plan's profession when the business is in India. It is never stored and is in no digest.
  - The fake engine adds the same guide on `venture.blueprint.get`, `.update` and `.draft` (`fake-price-ranges.ts`). `fixtures-start.json` was regenerated from the engine, and the existing pin test plus the new 6C3 test hold it equal.
- **UI** `app/ui/src/parts/PriceSources.tsx`, which follows the OnboardB reference.
  - The (i) button, named "Where this range comes from" (or "Why there is no range"), with aria-expanded and aria-controls. It is described by the range's own label, so several buttons are told apart.
  - The popover shows the range label, the sources as links that open apart from Chausar, the note, and "Checked {date}. Your price is yours to set." Escape closes it and returns focus to the button.
  - Phrasing elements only, so it sits inside a `<p>`.
  - `PriceRange` draws one line and `PriceGuideList` draws a profession. Gallery uses them for the sourced pay line and in the fold.
  - Prices (`PricesView`) shows a "Price ranges" fold under the offers. It is folded to stay inside the business/prices word budget, which only shrinks.
  - Ink rule helper: `[data-sourced-range]` is a third kind of proof, beside a receipt and a model estimate. Money inside it passes only if it holds its sources button.

## Research (1 November 2026)

Four research helpers fetched every source with WebFetch and quoted the figure. I re-fetched these myself as spot checks:
- the UrbanPro class 10 page (₹300 to ₹400 per hour);
- the ICAI scale PDF via pdftotext (dated 11.02.2020: ITR 8,000/6,000/4,000 and above; GST returns base plus 4,000/3,000/2,000 a month; incorporation 35,000/25,000/18,000; annual return 10,000/7,000/5,000);
- the NTM translation rates PDF (₹0.50 to ₹1 a word);
- Urban Company electricians (₹49 consultation);
- Tata 1mg home physiotherapy (₹800 or ₹900 a session).

Urban Company "starts at" prices move between fetches (one switch repair read ₹69 and ₹89 on different fetches), so those rows are labelled starting prices.

I left out these sources and marked the professions no reliable data:
- Contra's rate copy (it reads as generic, AI-style text);
- one studio's own podcast price page;
- LBB write-ups of single shops;
- NoBroker user-forum answers;
- PayScale freelance technical writers (nine profiles);
- the 2008 and 2012 government caps for tax return preparers;
- Editage's own price list;
- IndiaMART rows with unclear units (tender filing "per hour") or a different service (ISO certificates for SOPs).

Upwork, Fiverr, Superprof and most Indian news sites refused the fetcher (403), which is the main reason the freelance rows are empty. A run from a normal browser could fill several of them. Percent-only norms (broker 1 to 2 percent, the old COA architect scale, recruiter 8.33 percent of CTC) are not amounts, so those professions are no reliable data too.

## Deviations

- After cold review, the ways for the CA practice and translation keep their model estimate. ICAI's figures are minimums for single tasks, not the CA way's monthly service, and the government per-word rates differ from the way's per-video-minute unit and sit at the market's low end. Both show their guide in the fold.
- The Prices guide is a fold, not open at rest, because the word budget only shrinks.
- `priceGuide` is not on `venture.preview` or the 6C2 About-you preview, because those screens are being rebuilt in parallel. They can call `priceGuide(profession)` and render `PriceGuideList` once 6C2 or 6E6 knows the profession.
- Codes: errors use the declared `INVALID_VENTURE_REQUEST`, since the venture-core test allows only declared codes.

## Open items

- Most of the 27 covered professions (teaching, local services, licensed work) have no playbook yet. Their guides appear once 6E6 maps professions to the lesson, booking, document and sign-off desks: add each to `WAY_PRICES` or pass the profession from 6C2.
- The ICAI scale is linked through a caalley.com reprint, because icai.org's PDF would not load. Swap in ICAI's own link when it is reachable.
- Re-check the data before beta: marketplace prices move, and `PRICES_CHECKED_ON` and every range's `checkedOn` must change together.
- The founder may want the dropped marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Superprof) read from a normal browser to fill freelance rows.
