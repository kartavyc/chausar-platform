# 6C2: About you, A that becomes B (D15, D16, D16a)

## What was built

- Step 2 of the first run (`app/ui/src/places/onboarding/AboutYou.tsx`, from `OnboardAB`, `OnboardA`, `OnboardB`):
  - A: the message box (with attach for a resume PDF or LinkedIn export ZIP), the country chips (from the time zone, never the language) with Change, and the eleven field tiles plus "Something else".
  - Send turns A into B at once: the chat on the left rises and "Your business" slides in from the right, both in the same render, one motion of `--t-settle` (400 ms). Under reduced motion `--t-settle` is 0 and the component also sets `data-motion="none"`. Nothing changes while typing.
  - B: the thread, a reply box (reads again with the combined words), the AI country suggestion with Use/Keep (6A3 behaviour moved here), and the live preview: the engine preview's name, the field selector (the correction path), offers without any amount, "You set every price yourself", Keep this plan.
  - A tile goes straight to the plan (no chat); "Back to fields" returns to A with the text kept.
  - Keep this plan hands the idea and country to `Start` in a new `plan` mode: no box, the same vault steps the engine forces (passphrase where needed, recovery code once), then `onKept`, which saves the field (`venture.owner.set { familyId }`) and opens the 6C7 step.
- Engine:
  - `core/families.ts`: the 11 families of D2 with tiles, code-owned offers, keyword lists and a plan sentence each. It imports nothing from the engine (the fake reads the same file). `core/family-plan.ts` binds the engine's `classifyIdea`.
  - 6E1 hook: families map onto existing service types (teaching and licensed to consulting, local services to other, documents and people to consulting through the tender and job-search playbooks). The TODO at the top of `families.ts` says what to change; the test holds every plan sentence to its family's type.
  - `familyIdea`: the owner's words when they already plan as the family; else the family's plan sentence then the owner's words; else the plan sentence. Owner words are never rewritten.
  - `core/about.ts`: `venture.about.read { text?, attachment? }` (approve, app only, draft desk) with a strict schema (`chausar-owner-about-v1`): a listed family, up to three offers, a listed country and evidence copied from the owner words. Offers with a digit, a currency sign or a link are refused. A LinkedIn link in the text is refused with how to get the export. A resume PDF goes through the trusted PDF helper (passed to the draft desk); a LinkedIn ZIP gives only Profile.csv's headline, summary, industry and location (`core/profile-csv.ts`); the app repacks only Profile.csv before sending (`onboarding/linkedin.ts`). A file goes as the owner's dropped file (screened, previewed, recorded once). With no AI connected: the keyword guess and the family's own offers.
  - `venture.about.pick { familyId, text? }` and `venture.about.families {}` (no AI).
  - `OwnerProfileRecord.familyId` (optional), set through `venture.owner.set`; the owner view shows it only once set, so older profiles read exactly as before.
  - Shapes live in `core/first-run-types.ts` (no imports) so `contracts.ts` and `onboarding-contracts.ts` do not pull the engine into the app's type graph.

## Deviations

- Offers are not priced here: price ranges are 6C3; the preview says prices are set after keeping.
- The mic button in the reference is left out (voice is 6I1), and so are the quick reply chips: nothing that cannot work yet.
- The idea text decides the plan everywhere (`pickPlaybook(venture.idea)`), so the family reaches the plan through the idea sentence and not a new field on the venture.
- The fake engine uses its own keyword classifier when choosing between owner words and the plan sentence, so for Documents and People the idea text can differ from the engine's (the engine also matches flagship playbooks).
- `connections.line` was shortened ("Your AI and your tools.") to pay for the 6C7 link and keep the Connections word budget.
