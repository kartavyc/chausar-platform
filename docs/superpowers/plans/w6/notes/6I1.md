# 6I1 Push-to-talk (D45)

## What was built

- **Engine** (`platform/runtime/venture/agents/talk.ts`, shared parts in the browser-safe `agents/talk-core.ts`). Six methods, owner `agents`:
  - `venture.talk.get` (read): the notice with its digest, whether it was agreed and whether recordings are kept, the chosen service, whisper.cpp's state, Sarvam (label `fixture-tested`, its dated price, the owner's monthly limit and this month's spend, all code-computed in paise), reading aloud (`write` or `planned: write`), the open session, kept recordings. Hosted: `desktop: false` and "Push-to-talk works only in the desktop app."
  - `venture.talk.consent` (approve, app only): `{ digest, keepAudio }` agrees by the notice's digest (a changed notice is `APPROVAL_STALE`); `{ withdraw: true }` turns the microphone off and erases every kept recording.
  - `venture.talk.setup` (approve, app only): whisper.cpp, or Sarvam with the key (kept on the secret shelf as `speech:sarvam-stt`, never returned), the owner's monthly limit in paise (default ₹100, an owner setting) and the approval of Sarvam's dated rate by its digest; `{ clearSarvam: true }` removes the key.
  - `venture.talk.start` (prepare, app only, desktop only): needs the agreed notice and a ready service; opens one recording session for 60 seconds.
  - `venture.talk.hear` (approve, app only, desktop only): the session's one recording (mono 16 kHz WAV, up to 20 seconds, checked by `caption-audio.ts`). whisper.cpp: the existing `SpeechService.transcribeFile` (the `venture.transcription.set` setup) in a temp folder that is removed in `finally`. Sarvam: `POST https://api.sarvam.ai/speech-to-text`, header `api-subscription-key`, multipart `model=saaras:v4`, `language_code=unknown`, `file`; reads `transcript`. Its cost is `ceil(ms × 49 / 60000)` paise (₹0.49 a minute, read on sarvam.ai/api-pricing on 2026-11-01), counted against the limit inside the transaction before sending; a failed or quota answer keeps the cost counted and is never retried. The words come back for the chat box; Chausar keeps a receipt row (`venture-talk-heard`: service, length, cost) and never the words; the audio only when the owner chose to keep it.
  - `venture.talk.say` (approve, app only): reads one stored chief answer aloud. `spokenFrom` speaks words as written unless the chief's own number guard (`modelNumbers` plus Hindi number words) finds a number, which becomes "a number, shown on screen"; a figure part by its receipt's display; an estimate part as "a model estimate, shown on screen". Quote first (speech-out's `speechQuote` with the narration rate digest), then `speechSay` on the owner's Google key; the audio is returned to play, not kept, and the line says it is an AI voice. Without the key: "read it on screen".
- **No spoken approval.** Nothing in the chain sends a message or decides a card: the words land in the box unsent, the owner checks and sends them like typed words (the chat's own consent still applies), and approvals stay taps. A test checks that a heard "yes" changes no record but the talk receipt.
- **Desktop microphone.** `app/desktop/caption-permission.cjs` takes a one-use, 30-second lease from a `venture.talk.start` reply and re-checks it with the engine (`verifyTalk` in `main.cjs`: desktop, notice agreed, same open session) before Electron's permission handler grants audio; never video, never a reusable grant; cleared on hear, on withdraw and by the existing engine-down, window-closed and Forget paths.
- **App.** `places/chats/PushToTalk.tsx` under the chat box: a microphone button (hold, or hold Space or Enter), the notice with "Keep my recordings" (off) before the first recording, the engine's line after. `talk-microphone.ts` records with the existing caption worklet and turns the microphone off at 20 seconds or on release. `ReadAloud.tsx` on each chief answer: a play button, then "Read aloud for USD ..." from the engine's quote. Icon-only buttons with labels, so the chats word budget is unchanged (passes at 150). Strings in `text/en/chats.json`.
- **Fake engine** (`engine/fake-talk.ts`): the same checks, codes, words and views from `talk-core.ts`; it recognizes nothing ("Rehearsal words. No audio was recognized.").

## Deviations and why

- **The pinned method counts are not changed** (`venture-contracts.test.ts` and `venture-method-hints.test.ts`, 357 and 337). 6I1 adds 6 methods, all reached through the hosted host (start and hear refuse there). Other helpers add methods at the same time, so the integrator sets one number.
- **Spoken words replace what was in the box.** The Composer is keyed by its words handed over, so the transcript replaces any half-typed text. Appending needs a Composer change outside this package.
- **The fake's read-aloud quote is approximate** (Google's list price, its own rate digest) and the fake makes no audio: with the quote seen it answers `SPEECH_PLANNED` where the engine would return audio.
- **Sarvam's rate and limit are approved once** when the key is added, as standing configuration, not per recording. Each recording's cost is shown after it is heard.
- **Read aloud uses Google only**: the Sarvam Bulbul call in speech-out.ts stays planned (no price there yet).
