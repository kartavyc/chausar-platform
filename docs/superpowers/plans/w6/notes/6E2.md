# 6E2 Lesson desk (D2, D21, D23, D24)

## What was built

- **Playbook `lesson-desk`** ("Lesson desk for tutors and teachers"), service type `teaching` (6E1), registered in `playbooks/registrations.ts`, `definitions.ts`, `desks.ts`, `desk-types.ts`. Kit definition in `playbooks/lesson-desk/definition.ts`: four owner-priced offers (monthly classes, pilotable; worksheet pack; progress reports; exam preparation), roles, five steps (gather, plan, draft, approve, send), floor approvals ("Approve each exact parent message", "You send every parent message yourself"), honest connectors (mail app and WhatsApp `handoff`, UPI link on invoices `handoff`), three house rules (no locked rules, so nothing is held for attestation), a `figures-from-chausar` draft check, judge rules, recipe, boundaries, practice clients and a committed baseline (`baselines/lesson-desk.json`, 4 of 4). Offers, intake questions and registry data live in the import-free `meta.ts`, read by both the definition and the fake engine.
- **Desk** (`venture.desk.list/get/act`, kind `batch`, one record kind `venture-lesson-batch`). Pure transitions in `lesson-desk/core.ts`, shared with the fake: batches (days, time, length), learners (name, under 18, parent or guardian client, fee order, consent, phone), classes with present/late/absent marks, worksheets and quizzes with an answer key, quiz scores counted by code, attendance counted by code ("Attended 2 of 3 classes."), progress notes whose figures come only from those counts, parent updates, Remove (deletes the learner with every mark, score and message).
- **Children's data hard stop (D21)**, reusing the existing machinery, no parallel consent: the parent is the order's client, so the existing client AI permission gates every job at dispatch; each child is a person whose consent is the existing person consent record (`venture-voice-consent`, `kit/voice-consent.ts`, a recorded statement from the parent). The intake `learners` field is kind `people`, so `requireOrderPeopleConsent` checks children named on an order at intake, at every run start and at Accept. The desk's AI share (`lesson-desk/context.ts`, wired into `agent-runtime/desk-context.ts`) refuses with `HARD_STOP` and the child-data line, naming the child, unless that child's own consent is current (another child's consent never counts; withdrawal stops it at once). The share holds names, topics attended and work titles only: no counts, no contact.
- **Fees** go through the existing orders and invoices: action `fee` drafts an invoice (whole or a 6D1 part) on the learner's order with `draftInvoiceIn`; issuing stays in Money with its exact approval. Fee rows show the invoice total as a figure with its receipt.
- **Parent messages** never send: action `handoff` takes the batch digest the owner saw, records the approval of the exact message digest in the action journal (`owner.handoff`), then returns a `mailto:` or `wa.me` link (shared portable builder `playbooks/kit/owner-message.ts`). A message already handed over opens again without a new journal entry.
- **Forget**: `lesson-desk/privacy.ts` `eraseLessons`, wired into `network/forget.ts`.
- **UI**: `places/work/desks/LessonDeskDesk.tsx` (ready), words in `text/en/pb-lesson.json`, intake words in `kit-intake-words.json`, label in `playbook-labels.json`. Counts and lines are the engine's; message words sit in `[data-content]`; amounts are `Figure`s with receipts.
- **Fake engine**: `engine/fake-lesson-desk.ts` runs the same core over in-memory batches, with fees through the fake money and consents from the fake voice consents; onboarding metadata in `engine/lesson-booking-metadata.ts` (plan picks in `fixtures-kit-plan-picks.json`).

## Deviations and why

- Parental consent is the existing person consent record, which needs an audio file of the parent's statement. That is real evidence, but a signed form or photo is not accepted yet (see open items). Whether it meets the legal test for a parent's checked consent is marked for a lawyer in the rule's limitations.
- One record kind per batch (learners, classes, work and messages inside it) keeps the shared files to one line each.
- The AI drafts words only; progress figures are added by code. A draft stating attendance, scores, marks or fees fails `figures-from-chausar`.
- Tile category `shops-and-firms`: `WayCategory` has no teaching category; 6E6's families show the family.
- Derived fixtures regenerated from the engine (they now include other helpers' playbooks in flight): `fixtures-start.json` ways and `fixtures-playbook-picks.json` (listWays and the registry, written with `JSON.stringify(x, null, 2)`), `landed-kit-rules-data.json` (`node scripts/generate-kit-rules.mjs`, then re-indented to the committed 2-space form). Rerun both after the last batch 5 package lands.
