# 6E3 Booking desk (D2, D23, D24, D25)

## What was built

- **Playbook `booking-desk`** ("Booking desk for local services"), service type `local-services` (6E1), registered beside the lesson desk. Kit definition in `playbooks/booking-desk/definition.ts`: four owner-priced offers (home visit, pilotable; appointment; event package; monthly plan), seven steps (intake, quote, slot, deposit, draft, approve, send), floor approvals, honest connectors (mail app, WhatsApp and UPI link `handoff`; calendar sync `planned: write` with who it waits on and the manual path), four house rules (no locked rules), a `no-amounts` draft check, judge rules, recipe, boundaries, practice clients and a committed baseline (`baselines/booking-desk.json`, 4 of 4). Offers, intake questions and registry data in the import-free `meta.ts`.
- **Desk** (kinds `slot` and `booking`; record kinds `venture-booking-slot`, `venture-booking`). Pure transitions in `booking-desk/core.ts`, shared with the fake:
  - Slots: start in the owner's own clock words, length, clash refused (`BOOKING_SLOT_CLASH`), close refused while held (`BOOKING_SLOT_TAKEN`). Slot state (open, held, booked, closed) is derived from the bookings, so a cancel or Forget frees it.
  - Bookings: intake (client, request, address, phone), quote, hold, release, confirm, done, cancel.
  - **Quote by code**: the booking links an open order of its client; the quote is that order's own price receipt (owner's price times quantity, existing code), shown as a figure with its receipt. One order serves one live booking.
  - **Deposit, never a new money path**: action `deposit` drafts an advance invoice (6D1 part, the percent the owner types) on the booking's order with `draftInvoiceIn`; `issue-deposit` calls the existing `venture.invoice.issue` with the invoice digest the owner saw (the journal's exact approval). The deposit's UPI link and amount come from the invoice's How to pay (6D2).
  - **Reminders**: fixed templates. An appointment reminder names the held slot ("5 Nov 2026 at 10:00"), the request and the address, and no amount. A deposit reminder needs an issued deposit with money due and carries the invoice balance formatted by code and its UPI link. The AI writes none of these words or numbers.
  - **Handoff**: as on the lesson desk, the booking digest, then the journal's `owner.handoff` on the exact message digest, then a `mailto:` or `wa.me` link. Chausar sends nothing.
- **Forget**: `booking-desk/privacy.ts` `eraseBookings` (bookings of a forgotten client go; their slots open again; invoices stay under the books' own rules), wired into `network/forget.ts`.
- **UI**: `places/work/desks/BookingDeskDesk.tsx` (ready), words in `text/en/pb-booking.json`.
- **Fake engine**: `engine/fake-booking-desk.ts` runs the same core; the quote reads the fake's order receipt, the deposit uses the fake money's prepare and issue, How to pay comes from the fake invoice view.

## Deviations and why

- The quote links an order the owner already priced instead of creating one from the desk, so the quote is always the order's own receipt and no price is typed twice.
- A payment reminder after the due date stays the existing `venture.invoice.remind` in Money; the desk's deposit reminder asks before the visit.
- Tile category `shops-and-firms` (no local services category in `WayCategory`).
