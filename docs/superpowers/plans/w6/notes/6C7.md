# 6C7: Connect what your business needs (D50)

## What was built

- After the plan is kept, the first run's last step (`app/ui/src/places/onboarding/BusinessNeeds.tsx`) lists the outside services this business would use, each with one line on why and an honest label (`write`, `read`, `handoff`, `planned`). A service that works today has "Set up", which opens its own connector sheet in Connections (the service's own sign-in or key guide). A planned one says what it waits on and the manual path meanwhile, and has no Set up. Every row has Skip; "Go to Today" leaves at any time.
- The same list is in Connections at `#/connections/needs`, with a quiet "Services you need" link at the top of Connections.
- Engine: `core/needs-data.ts` (pure, shared with the fake) and `core/needs.ts` with `venture.needs.list {}` (read): the family kept with the business (owner profile `familyId`), else the keyword guess from the idea, else a short general list. The payment rail follows the country (Razorpay for India; the country's listed rail elsewhere; none when there is none). Every item has `required: false`.
- Labels for connector cards are data checked against the cards' real modes by test, so a card that gains a live mode fails the test until the list is updated. Needs whose job is not built (Telegram alerts 6F6, Tailscale 6F7, Happenstance and Probe42 6F8, Sunra 6F9) say planned even where a card can do something else.
- Chausar ships no keys: nothing is connected by reading or skipping.
