# 6G4 Dated memory (D44)

## What was built

- **Dated facts** (`platform/runtime/venture/memory/facts-data.ts` pure, `memory/facts.ts` engine). A `venture-memory-fact` is one short thing Chausar remembers about the business or one client. Each fact has:
  - a topic and its words;
  - **since when** it is true (default today, never a day after today);
  - **what it replaced** (`replaces`, and `replacedBy` and `replacedAt` on the old one);
  - its **source**: the owner, or an AI, labelled "From AI. You have not checked it.";
  - its **visibility**: "You and your AI" or "Only you".
- **Replacing never deletes** (`venture.memory.replace`). The old fact stays, marked replaced and linked to its successor, under "Earlier facts". The new one names what it replaced and can't start before it. A topic is kept once per subject (`FACT_EXISTS`: replace it instead), and a replaced fact can't be replaced again (`FACT_REPLACED`).
- **Forget still erases** (`venture.memory.forget`, app only). The words of the fact and of every version of it (followed both ways) are erased. The records keep their ids, dates and links, so the chain shows something was there. Finished model calls that carried the words read "Erased: you asked Chausar to forget these words.". Forget of a person (`network/forget.ts` calls `eraseFactsNaming`) removes every fact about their client and every fact whose words name them or hold their email, phone or page address, with every version, and clears links to removed versions.
- **AI facts are labelled, and visibility decides what an AI sees.**
  - An AI caller (the scoped tool channel `mcp`, or any source-tainted context) may add a fact. It is labelled as from AI and kept as the owner's only, and it can't be set otherwise from that channel (`APP_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`).
  - An AI may replace only a fact an AI wrote. Its rewrite goes back to the owner's only.
  - An AI caller's `venture.memory.list` returns only current facts the owner lets an AI read, and a client's only while that client's AI permission stands (no earlier facts). An AI may add a client fact only with that permission.
  - `venture.memory.visibility` is the owner's, inside the app.
- **The AI job reads them** (`agent-runtime/job-memory.ts`, `factsForTurn`). Each AI job of an order gets, as background, the business's facts and its own client's that an AI may see. The key is added only when there are any, so existing job context is unchanged.
- **Agent notes stay as they are.** They are the owner's own words with change rows and undo (4B2). Dated facts sit beside them, on the business's "What the AI knows" page and in each client sheet.
- **UI**: `FactsSection.tsx` (shared): each current fact with its topic, its words (quoted), "since 1 Oct 2026", its source line and who may see it, and Replace (with a new since day), "Let my AI read it" or "Only me", and Forget (asks once more). Then "Add a fact" and "Earlier facts" (with "replaced on"), both folded. On Business > What the AI knows (a new tile, "What Chausar remembers") and in the client sheet. Text in `text/en/crm.json`.
- **Fake engine** (`engine/fake-memory.ts`): the engine's pure checks and views. The app is its only caller, so the AI caller's rules are tested on the engine side.

New methods (5): `venture.memory.list` (read), `venture.memory.add` and `venture.memory.replace` (prepare), `venture.memory.visibility` and `venture.memory.forget` (approve, app only). Owner `agents`.

## Deviations and why

- **AI-written facts start as the owner's only.** That way an AI never reads back what an AI wrote until the owner lets it. The owner's own facts default to "You and your AI". Both are owner settings per fact.
- **Forget of one fact erases every version.** The earlier versions hold nearly the same words; keeping them would defeat Forget.
- **No scoped MCP tool yet.** The engine takes an AI caller's facts (tested on the `mcp` channel), but the scoped toolbox's pinned tool names were not changed. Today the AI reads facts through job memory.
- **"Business context" had no code under that name.** The business's facts live on "What the AI knows", beside the knowledge sources.
