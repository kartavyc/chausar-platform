# 6D1 Configurable invoices (D23)

## What was built

- **Several invoices per order.** `venture.invoice.prepare` takes an optional `part`: `{ kind: 'advance' | 'milestone', percent, label? }`, `{ kind: 'partial', amount (before tax), label? }` or `{ kind: 'final' }`. With no part and nothing issued yet, it drafts the whole order exactly as before (same receipt, same digest), so every older flow is unchanged. With no part after parts were issued, it drafts the exact rest.
- **The cut** (`platform/runtime/venture/money/invoice-parts.ts`, portable, pure BigInt): an advance or milestone takes a share in basis points of the order's amount before tax, rounded half up; a part payment takes the typed amount before tax; tax on each part is worked out fresh at the order's rate (`price.ts`). The final invoice takes the exact rest of every figure (amount before tax, each tax line, total), so the invoices of an order add up to its receipt to the paisa. A share or amount that takes everything left becomes the final cut.
- **"One order becomes one invoice, once" is gone, safely.** Nothing may bill past the order: a cut whose amount before tax, any tax line or total passes the rest is refused (`ORDER_OVER_INVOICED`); an order billed in full refuses (`QUOTE_ALREADY_INVOICED`, new words: "This order is billed in full already. Change the order before you bill more."). There is no override: billing more needs the order changed first (no silent over-billing). Issuing re-checks at commit time: the parts issued since the draft must equal what the draft was cut against (`APPROVAL_STALE` otherwise), and a whole-order invoice cannot issue beside parts. One draft per order at a time (a new prepare replaces it).
- **Receipts.** Each part has its own price receipt (subject: the invoice, `inputs.part` naming the kind, share, the whole-order receipt it was cut from and what was billed before), with steps and a template explanation checked by the number guard ("Advance: 30% of the order's INR 19,750.00 before tax is INR 5,925.00, plus CGST ... = INR 6,991.50." / "Final invoice: the order's ... less ... billed before = ..."). Later parts reuse the receipt of the first issued part, so a price change never moves an order already part-billed.
- **Every invoice still needs exact approval**: unchanged journal + policy gate on the digest; part, terms and reminder days are inside the digest (only when present, so old digests are identical).
- **Due dates.** Default is **due in 7 days** (was 15), owner-editable in billing settings (`venture.billing.set { dueInDays }`). Orders take an optional `dueDate` at `venture.order.create`; the invoice is due then (when within 90 days) unless days are given.
- **Payment terms**: a terms line in billing settings, copied onto each invoice when drafted and printed on the client copy ("Terms: ...").
- **Reminder schedule**: the owner's default days late in billing settings (default 3, 10, 30), an invoice's own `reminderDays` at prepare, and a recurring schedule's own days still win over the default (`reminders.ts reminderDaysFor`).
- **Order status** becomes `invoiced` only when the order is billed in full. The view carries `billed` (order total, billed, left, from code) for part invoices; the client copy says "Order total ..., billed before ...".
- **Recurring invoices** are unchanged (they still draft whole-order invoices due in 15 days, their own constant).
- **Fake engine parity** (`app/ui/src/engine/fake-money.ts`, `fake-money-pay.ts`): parts through the engine's own `invoice-parts.ts`, the same codes and words, default 7 days, order due dates, terms, reminder days, the issue re-check. The fake's order snapshot now carries its tax treatment and rate (`fake-work.ts`).
- **UI**: on a draft invoice sheet, "Bill part of the order" (kind, percent or amount, name), sent as typed; an issued part shows its name and "Bill the rest"; the invoices list keeps a part-billed order under "to invoice" until its final part.

## Deviations and why

- **Default due changed from 15 to 7 days** as the brief asked. Tests that pinned the old default were updated where they test the default (venture-money "default 7 days", money-invoices, money-views); shared scenario helpers keep their worked-out dates by passing `dueInDays: 15` explicitly (`venture-goals-helpers.ts`, one call in venture-credits). Other suites that rely on the implicit default could still move; the integrator's shards will show them.
- No separate "edit an order's due date" method: the due date is set when the order is made, and each invoice's days can be set at prepare. Open item below.
- The engine handler count pin moved 43 to 47 (venture-money) and the method pins 316/297 to 320/301 (venture-method-hints).

## Open items

- An order's due date cannot be changed after the order is made (needs a small `venture.order.due` method if wanted).
- The order's "paid" stage on Work reads the newest issued invoice; for a part-billed order it can say done while the rest is unbilled.
- The fake's part receipt explanation leaves out the tax words the engine's has.
