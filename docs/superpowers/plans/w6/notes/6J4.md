# 6J4 Beta guide

## What was built

- `docs/beta-guide.md`: the beta guide for a non-technical owner in India. Plain short sentences, a contents list, ten parts: install (Windows "More info, then Run anyway"; Linux AppImage made executable, plus the FUSE 2 tip), connect your AI (Codex, Claude Code, key), the first business (About you, keep the plan, the services step, read and accept the plan, set prices, Practice then Go live), the first client and order (Add client, AI permission per client, New order, read, approve, send it yourself, Mark delivered), getting paid (issue, Bill part of the order, How clients pay set up, UPI QR, Copy UPI link, Save file, Share on WhatsApp, Email it, Record payment, Remind), phone access (summary plus a link to `docs/venture/phone-access.md`), Telegram alerts, sending a report, what Chausar never does, where to get help.
- Four screenshots of the fake engine at 1280 px, light theme, in `docs/beta-guide/`: `connect-your-ai.png`, `about-you.png`, `how-clients-pay.png`, `phone-access.png`. The QR codes in them hold only synthetic data (the UPI ID `yourname@okaxis`, and the fake engine's sample pairing link to 192.168.1.20).
