# 6D4 Foreign currency (D26), building on 6A3 and D15a

## What was built

- **An order in the client's own currency at the owner's own price.** `venture.order.create` takes `currency` and `unitPrice` together (typed in that currency, read exactly in its minor units; zero-decimal currencies work). Only for a client in another country, in a currency other than the business's own; the price must be above zero. Kept on the order as `foreignPrice`. **Nothing is converted**, ever.
- **Receipts** (`quote.ts buildPriceReceipt` with `foreign`): priced in that currency with the owner's price, `inputs.currencyBy: 'order'`. The business's plan currency check stays as it was.
- **GST export (LUT)**: the existing tax decision decides. An Indian business with its LUT confirmed bills the export zero-rated ("GST, zero-rated export under LUT", place of supply "Outside India (export of services)"), as the code already did in rupees. Without an LUT, IGST would be owed in rupees at an exchange rate, so a foreign-currency order is refused with the way out (`FOREIGN_NEEDS_LUT`: "Confirm your Letter of Undertaking (LUT) in your plan to bill this client in their currency, or bill them in INR."). Any other treatment with tax is refused the same way; a business that charges no tax on the sale can bill abroad in the client's currency.
- **Invoices** draft from the order's own price and currency (whole or in parts, 6D1), and the owner's view notes: "This invoice is in USD. Chausar does not convert it: record its INR value at the exchange rate of the invoice date in your tax return, or ask your accountant." Payments are typed and recorded in the invoice's currency. 6A3's per-currency calculator totals and historical currency handling already keep mixed currencies apart in the books.
- **How to pay**: UPI is rupees only, so a foreign invoice shows no UPI link ("UPI takes rupees only, so this invoice in USD shows your bank details instead.") and lists the bank details with the SWIFT code (6D2).
- **Fake engine parity** (`fake-work.ts` order create): the same fields, refusals and words; the fake's order snapshot carries the foreign currency and price.

## Deviations and why

- The owner types the foreign price per order rather than per offer: the plan keeps one price list in the business's currency (D15a), and a per-offer foreign price list would change the plan's digest and approval flow. Open item if a standing price list per currency is wanted.

## Open items

- The UI has no currency fields on the new-order form yet (orders made by the AI tool or the engine method carry them; the money screens show and bill them). The order form is outside the money places.
