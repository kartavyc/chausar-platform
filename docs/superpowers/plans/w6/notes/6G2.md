# 6G2 Retire an agent (D43), fixture-tested

## What was built

- **The check screen.** `venture.agents.retire.check { agent }` (read; `platform/runtime/venture/agents/retire.ts`) answers what the agent holds (its step titles, standing orders, notes, messages), what is in flight (runs on its steps that are working or waiting, its open question, note or instruction proposals waiting in its chat, Today cards it raised, a routine run of its parked on Today), the routines that are on and will switch off, who can take over (every other AI agent on the team, in team order; the owner always can as `owner`), the fixed promise of what is kept, `blocked` (the last AI agent cannot retire) and a digest of exactly that. Keeper and code roles answer `AGENT_IS_CODE`, an unknown id `AGENT_NOT_FOUND`, a retired one `AGENT_RETIRED`.
- **Retire.** `venture.agents.retire { agent, to, digest }` (approve, app only) is modelled on `venture.team.clearQuarantine`: it recomputes the check inside the transaction, refuses a changed one with `APPROVAL_STALE`, the last AI agent with `AGENT_LAST_ONE`, and any `to` that is not another AI agent on the team or `owner` with `INVALID_VENTURE_REQUEST`. It switches the agent's routines off (never removes them) and stores one `venture-agent-retirement` record (`agent, name, to, routines, digest, restoredAt`). Nothing else is written.
- **Handover.** A leaf module `agents/retired.ts` resolves who holds a role's steps now: the role itself, else the end of its handover chain (an agent on the team, or the owner). One harness runs every role's steps, so a handover changes whose card and chat show the steps (`views/team.ts`, `agents/roles.ts chatAgent`, the step check in `venture.team.gate`) and whose AI settings run new work on them (`delivery/runs.ts`: the native job's `agentId` is the holder, or the team's own `delivery` settings when the owner took over). A chain A to B, then B to C sends A's steps to C; Bring back of B puts them back with B.
- **Read-only shelf.** `venture.team.list` leaves a retired agent out of `agents` and adds `retired: RetiredAgentView[]` (only when someone is on it, so every existing shape is unchanged). `venture.agents.thread.list` still reads its thread and adds `retired: { at, line }`. `thread.send`, `notes.edit` and `routines.set` naming it answer `AGENT_RETIRED`. The chief no longer routes to it (`chief/routing.ts teamAgents`), and the routine runner already skips an agent not on the team.
- **Nothing lost.** Receipts, the journal and its chain, Today's proposals, the agent's proposals, notes and note changes are not written by retiring (the engine test snapshots all of them). A Today approval it raised is still listed and decidable; its drafted note proposal can still be decided; a kept note's change can still be undone; Forget still works on the shelf (DPDP). Exact-approval digests bind ids, not names, so nothing in the journal changes.
- **"(retired)".** Today cards and reports that name it read `Story editor (retired)`: proposal rows (`chief/rows.ts`), relay notices (`agents/relay.ts memberName`) and a routine's "It asks" name (`routines.ts askedName`). One helper, `retiredName`, is exported for 6G5's per-agent spend lines.
- **Bring back.** `venture.agents.restore { agent }` (approve, app only) clears the retirement (the record stays, with `restoredAt`) and switches on again the routines retiring switched off that are still its own, still there and still off, after the same connection check `routines.set` asks for (`ROUTINE_NEEDS_CHECK`, atomic: a refused Bring back changes nothing). The team list then matches the one before retiring, step for step.
- **Words** live in `agents/retired-words.ts` (no imports), shared by the engine and the fake engine.
- **UI.** Agent panel, Instructions tab: a quiet "Retire this agent" last. It opens the check in place of the tab (`places/team/AgentRetire.tsx AgentRetire`): what it holds, in flight, routines that switch off, a "Who takes over" radio group (other agents and You), the kept line, Cancel and one primary "Retire {name}". The last agent shows the engine's reason and no Retire button. After retiring, the panel shows the agent read only (`RetiredPanel`): shelf line, Bring back, its chat history with no composer and its notes with Forget and Undo but no Add or Edit (`AgentNotes readOnly`). Team shows a quiet Retired shelf under the departments (`RetiredShelf`) with "Read its chat" and "Bring back {name}", only when someone is retired, so Team's words at rest are unchanged.
- **Fake engine.** `app/ui/src/engine/fake-retire.ts` answers the three methods with the engine's codes, words, field checks and order; `fake.ts` shapes `venture.team.list` through it (retired agents out, handed steps to the holder, the shelf), adds "(retired)" to Today rows naming a retired agent, and wires the retired lookup into `fake-agents.ts` (read-only thread, `AGENT_RETIRED` on send and note edits) and `fake-4j22a.ts` (routines).

## Live check

Fixture-tested: retiring touches no installed tool or outside service. It only reads and writes records in the vault.

## Deviations and why

- **Routines switch off; they are not handed over.** D43 lists routines among what the owner hands over or stops, and also says "routines switch off". I followed the stricter reading in the brief: every routine of the agent that is on switches off, and Bring back switches exactly those on again. The owner can point a routine at another agent in Business > Routines.
- **Handover to the owner** means no AI card shows those steps and new work on them runs under the team's own AI settings (the harness still drafts; every outside action still asks the owner). Chausar has no person-step mode to move an AI step to, so it does not claim one.
- **Today card digests.** A card that names the retired agent changes its words, so its row digest changes and a tap made from the old card answers stale once ("here is the new version"); nothing is lost and the card stays decidable. The fake engine keeps its stored row digest (it cannot recompute a digest the client would match), so only the name differs there.
- **Bring back has no digest.** It restores a recorded state; it is approve level and app only.

## Open items

- **Fake engine recursion (6G5 interaction, please confirm).** Any routine that names an agent made the fake engine recurse without end: `venture.routines.list` words the routine with the team's names (`venture.team.list`), the team reads the spend policy (6G5 meters), and the policy reads `venture.routines.list` again (`fake-extras.ts routinesOf`). Its `try` swallowed the stack overflow, so the worker spun for minutes. I added a small re-entrancy guard in `fake-extras.ts routinesOf` (a nested read answers an empty list to the inner, discarded team view). 6G5 may prefer another fix.

- `agents/capabilities.ts`, `persona.ts`, `recall.ts` and `models/roles.ts` still read the playbook's own role of a step, so the receiving agent's capability page and per-role AI settings do not list handed steps. Harmless (the harness runs all steps) but worth a follow-up.
- 6G5 (spend meter per agent) should name a retired agent with `retiredName` from `agents/retired.ts`.
- Method counts in `venture-method-hints`, `venture-contracts` and `venture-engine` need the integrator's single update (this package adds three methods, all reached through the hosted host).
