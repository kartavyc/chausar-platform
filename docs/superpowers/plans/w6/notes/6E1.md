# 6E1: Service types for teaching, local services and licensed work (D2)

## What was built

- **Three new service types** in `contracts.ts` `SERVICE_TYPES`: `teaching`, `local-services`, `licensed`. They sit after `support` and before `other`, so a keyword tie still goes to the older type and old ideas keep their type. Labels: Teaching and coaching, Local service business, Licensed practice. Second offers: hourly, project, project.
- **Template words** for each in `core/service-types.ts`: questions, two offers in all three pricing models, four steps (step three drafts), two approvals, customer and one-liner.
  - The licensed approvals keep the professional as the one who signs.
  - The teaching approvals ask a parent before a child's details are used (D21).
- **Keywords** moved to a pure file, `core/service-keywords.ts`. The engine's classifier and the fake engine (`app/ui/src/engine/fixtures.ts`) both read it now, so they guess alike. Each new type has its own words.
- **Every table keyed on a service type** knows the new types, so nothing falls through:
  - brief template work (`build/brief.ts`);
  - departments (`playbooks/departments.ts`: teaching production, local services operations, licensed production);
  - agent names (`views/shared.ts`);
  - required sections and template outlines (`delivery/template-outline.ts`);
  - practice clients (`delivery/scenarios.ts`, mirrored in `fake-practice.ts`);
  - the AI interview schema, which now reads `SERVICE_TYPES` instead of a copied list (`core/interview.ts`);
  - UI `types.ts`, `SERVICE_LABELS` and `business.kind.*` in `common.json`.
  - The grep also found `delivery/harness/dictionary.ts` (a partial table that falls back to none), `patches.ts`, `capabilities.ts` and `runs.ts`. Those switch on one old type only, so they need no change.
- **Families** (`core/families.ts`): teaching, local services and licensed plan as their own types, and `TODO(6E1)` is gone.
  - New plan sentences: "Tuition classes and lessons for students and adult learners", "Home visits, repairs and salon bookings for families nearby", "Client follow ups and review packs for a licensed professional to sign off".
  - Documents and people still plan as consulting, because D2 adds only three types.
- **Keyword fallbacks for every family**: each family's label and line finds that family, and so does each of the 57 profession names from 6E6. Two professions needed fixes:
  - "technical" added to documents, so Technical writer no longer lands in writing;
  - research's bare "data" became "data analys", so Data entry finds shop and back office.
