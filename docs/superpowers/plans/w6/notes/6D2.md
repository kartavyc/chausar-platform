# 6D2 How to pay (D24)

## What was built

- **Billing settings** (`platform/runtime/venture/money/billing.ts`, rules in portable `billing-rules.ts`): `venture.billing.get` (read) and `venture.billing.set` (approve, **app only**, so neither the AI tool nor the phone can change where clients pay). Fields: UPI ID (checked as a VPA: name of letters, digits, dot, hyphen or underscore, `@`, a letter handle; handle kept lower case; `INVALID_UPI_ID`), name clients see, bank details (account name, account number 6 to 34 letters or digits, IFSC `AAAA0XXXXXX` for India and/or SWIFT for abroad; `INVALID_BANK_DETAILS`), days until due, terms line, reminder days. Kept on the business record (`VentureRecord.billing`); absent fields read as defaults.
- **UPI link and QR** (`upi.ts`, `qr.ts`): `upi://pay?pa=&pn=&am=&cu=INR&tn=Invoice <number>&tr=<number>`, amount from the invoice's **exact balance** in paise formatted by code (`23305.00`), only for an **issued rupee** invoice with money left. The QR code is drawn by Chausar's own encoder (byte mode, level M, versions 1 to 10, Reed-Solomon, eight masks with the standard penalty) written for Chausar from ISO/IEC 18004; **no dependency added** (package.json has no QR library). The invoice view carries `howToPay` (UPI ID, link, QR matrix, amount, reference, the receipt id for the owner's mark; bank details; a plain note when UPI does not apply: a draft, or a non-rupee invoice).
- **Client copy**: a "How to pay" section (UPI ID, the UPI link with the amount, the payment link if any, bank details) after the totals, only when the owner set something up, so older documents are unchanged. **No receipt id** on the client copy, the PDF page or the share words (tested).
- **PDF and share** (`invoice-pdf.ts`, `venture.invoice.pdf`, prepare, app only, issued invoices only): the client copy plus the QR as SVG shapes, printed through the desktop's existing print port (`playbooks/kit/report/pdf.ts`); without the port (web, hosted, tests) the file is the same page as HTML and says "A PDF needs the desktop app." The answer carries share links that open the owner's own apps: `https://wa.me/?text=...` and `mailto:<client email>?subject=...&body=...`. The owner attaches the file and sends; Chausar sends nothing.
- **Paid by UPI stays the owner's word**: a UPI payment the owner records shows on the timeline as "Paid ... by UPI on ..., marked paid by you" (engine and fake). A bank row match does not carry those words.
- **UI**: the invoice sheet's "How clients pay" block (the engine's QR drawn as squares, dark on light in both themes via two new tokens `--qr-light`/`--qr-dark`; the UPI ID; the amount beside its receipt mark; Copy UPI link; bank details; Save file, Share on WhatsApp, Email it), and a "How clients pay" sheet (`BillingSheet.tsx`, sheet `billing:settings`) with the settings form. Everything is sent as typed (days too); the engine reads and checks it.
- **Fake engine parity**: billing settings with the engine's own checks and words, `howToPay` with the engine's own link and QR builder, the HTML file with the same note and share words.

## Live check

- **Real QR, decoded back, no payment.** The engine's own `upiPayment` for INR 1.00 to a synthetic UPI ID (`chausar.livecheck@okaxis`, reference INV-9001) was rendered two ways and decoded with OpenCV 5.0.0: (1) the matrix rasterised directly, (2) the exact SVG the PDF page uses, rendered by the installed **Electron 44.4.5** (Chromium capturePage). Both decoded to the identical link, `pa=chausar.livecheck@okaxis`, `pn=Chausar Live Check`, `am=1.00`, `cu=INR`, `tr=INV-9001`. Evidence: `results/w6-6D2-live-qr.json`, `results/w6-6D2-live-electron.json`. The encoder was also checked across versions 1 to 10 and all eight masks (every symbol decoded by at least one of OpenCV's two detectors).
- Not done: opening the QR in a phone UPI app (no phone on this machine). Nothing was paid; no message was sent. The disposable Electron profile was deleted; no Electron process was left running.

## Deviations and why

- The billing settings live in a Money sheet ("How clients pay"), reached from the invoice sheet, not on the Settings place: Settings is another package's screen, and this keeps the Money word budgets unchanged (no new words at rest).
- The receipt mark beside the UPI amount is the invoice's price receipt, as the existing figures card does for the balance; after a part payment the balance is explained by that receipt and the payments' books receipts in the card above.
