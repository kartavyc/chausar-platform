# 6E5: Sign-off desk (D2, D21)

## What was built

- **The sign-off gate, made general** (`playbooks/kit/signoff-core.ts`, pure). It holds the papers digest, the owner-edit rule and the stage machine (not-needed, outline, no-signer, not-asked, unsent, sent, returned, changed, signed), plus the block words. All of it was moved out of the CA firm's gate.
  - `ca-firm/signoff-gate.ts` now runs on it. Its stages (`no-ca`) and words are unchanged.
  - `playbooks/kit/signoff-gate.ts` adds `regulatedSignoff` and `regulatedSignoffBlock`: the CA gate first, then the sign-off desk.
  - Every former `caSignoffBlock` call site now asks it: Accept (`delivery/runs.ts`, which throws `CA_SIGNOFF_REQUIRED` or `SIGNOFF_REQUIRED`), the run view, share links, posting, report share, the Today edit and refusal checks, and the runs view.
- **Playbook `signoff-desk`** (`playbooks/signoff-desk/`), `serviceType: 'licensed'`:
  - Offers: legal drafts (advocate), patient follow-ups (doctor), review packs (SEBI-registered adviser), secretarial papers (company secretary), tax and payroll papers (CA).
  - `professions.ts` is pure and the fake reads it too. Each profession has:
    - a signer and a registration number with its format (Bar Council enrolment, medical registration, SEBI INA or INH, ICSI ACS or FCS, ICAI six digits);
    - the tools refused in code;
    - the draft words its regulator forbids;
    - its rules.
  - `desk.ts` has two kinds:
    - `professional`: name the professional of record for each profession;
    - `paper`: list regulated drafts, then send, sign or return. Signing needs the registration number of the professional the papers were sent to (`SIGNOFF_SIGNER_MISMATCH`). The owner's edit is new papers that wait for their own signature.
  - In a sign-off desk business every client output is regulated. An offer the owner added takes the plan's first desk profession.
- **Regulator limits, refused in code. No setting or attestation unlocks them.**
  - **Tools.** `core/compliance.ts` `refuseRegulatorForbidden` also reads `signoffDeskSwitchOffs`. For advocates and doctors, outreach (outreach draft and open, lead drafts) and promotional posts are refused with `REGULATOR_FORBIDS`, even when no compliance answer is recorded.
  - **Words.** The kit check `regulator-limits` fails a draft, which blocks Accept, when it holds:
    - an AI diagnosis or prescription (doctor);
    - a solicitation or promised outcome (advocate);
    - a stock tip, target price or promised return (financial planner).
- **Rules, marked honestly.** Read on the regulator's own page on 1 November 2026: NMC Code of Medical Ethics Regulations 2002, clauses 6.1.1 (soliciting patients) and 1.4.1 (registration number on prescriptions), at https://www.nmc.org.in/page/rules-regulations-rules-regulations-of-erstwhile-mci-code-of-medical-ethics-regulations-2002. Unchecked, with the official page Chausar tried:
  - BCI Rule 36: the page needs JavaScript;
  - Telemedicine Guidelines 2020: mohfw.gov.in answered 403;
  - SEBI IA 2013 and RA 2014: sebi.gov.in answered 403;
  - Company Secretaries Act 1980: the page shows no text;
  - Chartered Accountants Act 1949: the PDF loaded, but its text could not be read.

  In the kit register these read `official` or `unconfirmed`, and the desk shows "Not checked against the official page yet".
- **Forget** removes a client's requests and what was sent and decided on them. The professional of record stays.
- **Shared files** (small additions only):
  - engine: `contracts.ts` (`venture-signoff`, error codes), `records.ts` (id prefix), `build/posting.ts` (refusal code), `views/queue-actions.ts` (`signoffEdit` words), `network/forget.ts`
  - registries: `registrations.ts`, `definitions.ts`, `desks.ts`, `desk-types.ts`
  - fake engine: `fake-work.ts` (Accept gate), `fake.ts` and `fake-playbooks.ts` (the `signoffBlock` route)
  - test: `venture-professions-w6.test.ts` (TYPE_PENDING emptied, as 6E1 asked).
- **UI**: `SignoffDeskDesk.tsx` (words in `pb-signoff.json`), the fake `fake-signoff-desk.ts` and the metadata file `signoff-metadata.ts`.

## Deviations

- I read "no AI client calls for advocates" as no AI contact with prospective clients. In Chausar that means the outreach and lead tools and promotional posts, which are now refused for advocates and doctors. There is no AI phone-calling tool in a sign-off desk business to switch off.
- A signature is recorded inside Chausar by the professional typing their number on the owner's computer, the same model as the CA firm. It is not a digital signature certificate.
- There are no Today rows for sign-off desk papers; the desk itself is the place to send and sign.
