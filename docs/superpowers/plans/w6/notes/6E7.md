# 6E7: Traders (D49, D21)

## What was built

- **Securities guard in code** (`platform/runtime/venture/policy/securities-guard.ts`). It rests on the existing `securities-advice` hard stop, so no setting or attestation unlocks it. It finds trade advice, ratings, target prices and stop losses, promised returns, tips, trade calls and copy trading. It leaves ordinary business words and journal facts alone ("buy now and get a free consultation", "you sold shares in March"). Hooks:
  - `records.ts` `modelWords`: every model text that becomes a draft, a judge note, a brief, a question or a reply. The refusal is `MODEL_OUTPUT_REJECTED`, so model callers fall back as they already do.
  - `agents/answer.ts` `validateAnswer`: an agent chat answer with advice is refused, and the template answer is used.
  - `core/about.ts`: About-you offers that sell tips, calls or copy trading are refused.
  - `agent-runtime/external-config.ts`: saving an owner MCP server on a broker trading host is refused with `HARD_STOP`. This covers a local bridge whose arguments point at one (for example `mcp-remote https://mcp.kite.trade/mcp`).
  - `agent-runtime/external-mcp.ts`: a server whose tool list can place, change or cancel orders is refused with `HARD_STOP` before listing or calling, even if the owner would approve. Tools are spotted by name, description or order fields.
- **Trading journal core** (`playbooks/trading-journal/`).
  - `brokers.ts` reads Zerodha, Groww, Upstox and Dhan tradebook CSVs into exact rows. Value is in ten-thousandths of a rupee and any precision past four places is refused. It finds the header row below any preamble and dedupes on day, contract, side and trade id.
  - `journal.ts` handles the rest:
    - Splits equity into intraday and delivery legs.
    - Works every charge per day, exchange and leg kind: brokerage per executed order, STT, exchange charges, investor protection charge, SEBI fee, stamp duty, DP charges per scrip per sell day, and GST. All BigInt, rounded half up to the paisa.
    - Works realised P&L first in, first out. An option with a known expiry that is still open closes at zero on expiry.
    - Applies the grandfathering cost from an owner-entered fair market value. A long-term lot without one is flagged and left out of the totals.
  - `rates.ts` holds the rates as versioned data. Each has its source URL, start day and how it was read, checked 1 November 2026: STT from 1 October 2024, and the Finance Act 2026 F&O STT from 1 April 2026; exchange charges, with the protection charge folded in from 1 March 2026; SEBI fee, stamp duty, GST; each broker's brokerage and DP charges.
  - `tax-rules.ts` covers financial year 2025-26 (Income-tax Act 1961: 43(5), 111A, 112A, 55(2)(ac), 2(42A)) and tax year 2026-27 (Income-tax Act 2025: 66(31) and 26(3), 66(33), 196, 198, 90(7) and 90(8)(b), 2(101)).
  - `tax-pack.ts` produces figures for intraday (speculative), F&O (non-speculative), STCG and LTCG with the limit, and delivery STT and other charges. Each figure carries its rule, Act and year. Rates are shown as the rule's rate and never applied to give a tax to pay. Labelled "Check with your CA".
  - `receipt.ts` writes the work receipts for the journal and the pack.
- **Playbook**:
  - Registration, kit definition (`definition-data.ts`, valid under `definePlaybook`), dated rules (`rules.ts`), the `no-securities-advice` check and the baseline (four cases, scored by the real guard and calculator).
  - Desk `trading-journal` / kind `journal`, with list and get. Its actions are `import`, `add-opening` and `tax-pack`. Each saves a `venture-trade-journal` record and a `venture-work-receipt`.
  - Joined into `registrations.ts`, `definitions.ts`, `desks.ts` and `desk-types.ts`, and in `contracts.ts`: the record kind and type, and the errors `TRADEBOOK_INVALID`, `TRADEBOOK_DUPLICATE`, `OPENING_INVALID`, `JOURNAL_EMPTY`, `TAX_YEAR_UNSUPPORTED`. `records.ts` has the id prefix.
- **UI and fake engine**:
  - `places/work/desks/TradingJournalDesk.tsx` and its CSS module.
  - `text/en/pb-trading.json`.
  - `engine/fake-trading-journal.ts` runs the engine's own `desk-core`, `journal` and `receipt`, so codes, words, figures and receipts match.
  - The kit intake words and the playbook label were added.

## Deviations

- **Unchecked broker formats.** Groww, Upstox and Dhan publish no column list and no public sample was found. Their parsers follow public descriptions, are labelled `checked: false`, and the import line says so. A real anonymised export from each is needed to confirm. The Zerodha format is checked against public samples. Zerodha's `EQ` segment value for cash trades was not seen in a sample, so equity is accepted whenever the segment is not F&O.
- **Rates marked as read.**
  - STT is `official`, from the BSE notice; the NSE circular timed out.
  - Exchange charges, SEBI fee, stamp duty and GST are `secondary`, quoted by brokers.
  - The ICAI turnover method is `unchecked`.
  - Brokerage pages show no start day, so the current rates apply to every checked day, and the rule says so.
  - Trades before 1 October 2024 are refused rather than guessed. Older holdings go in as opening holdings.
- **Rounding.** Charges are rounded per contract note line to the paisa. Brokers may round STT and stamp duty to the rupee, and the receipt says to compare with contract notes.
- **The journal holds only the owner's own trades.** No client data means no Forget path was needed. The kit requires an offer, so the one offer is `audience: 'internal'` and `pilotable: true`. The validator demands one pilotable offer; a pilot does not really fit an internal offer.
- **The tile's "clients pay" range** is a labelled model estimate the kit requires. For an owner's own journal it reads oddly. This is for the founder.
- **Shared fixtures regenerated.** I regenerated `app/ui/src/engine/fixtures-playbook-picks.json` and the `ways` in `fixtures-start.json` from the engine. Both now also carry 6E4's document desk and 6E6's `families` field, which were missing.
- **6E6's test.** I removed `trading-journal` from `EXPECTED_PENDING` in `platform/tests/venture-professions-w6.test.ts`, as the test asks once the desk lands. That file still fails on `document-desk` (6E4).
- **Playbook label count.** `app/ui/tests/playbook-labels.test.ts` now expects 28.
