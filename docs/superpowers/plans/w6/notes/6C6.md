# 6C6: Day-job and compliance check (D5, D21)

## What was built

- **Data** (`platform/runtime/venture/core/compliance-data.ts`, pure, shared with the fake): the side-work question "Does your employment contract allow side work? This is your responsibility.", the line that Chausar does not read the contract, and per-profession rules, regulator switch-offs and own hard stops for: advocate (BCI Rule 36: outreach and promotional posts off), doctor (Telemedicine Guidelines 2020), financial planner or MFD (SEBI adviser and analyst rules), school teacher (RTE s.28), architect (CoA title), real estate broker (RERA), home chef (FSSAI), physiotherapist (NCAHP), call centre agent (TRAI). A family id from 6C2 (`teaching`, `licensed`...) also answers, with no profession-specific rules. Every statute is marked `checked: false` and the screen says "Not checked against the official page yet" (6E6 checks them).
- **Engine** (`core/compliance.ts`): `venture.compliance.get { profession? }` (read; also answered before any vault exists, from `engine.ts`, so onboarding can show it at the moment the profession is picked); `venture.compliance.set { profession, sideWork, digest }` (approve, app only) records a `venture-compliance` record with the date and the switch-offs. `refuseRegulatorForbidden` refuses with REGULATOR_FORBIDS in `venture.outreach.draft`, `venture.outreach.open`, `venture.leads.draft` (outreach) and `venture.posting.propose` (advertising). It reads both the recorded switch-offs and the profession's current data, so no setting or attestation turns it back on; the message ends "No attestation or setting can unlock it."
- **Component for onboarding** (`app/ui/src/parts/ComplianceCheck.tsx`): `<ComplianceCheck profession="advocate" record onDone={answer => ...} />`. Radio answers (Yes, No, I am not sure), the responsibility line, the profession's rules with the unchecked tag, "Switched off by your regulator", its own hard stops, and Continue (locked until answered). With `record` (default) it calls `venture.compliance.set`; with `record={false}` (before the business exists) it hands back `{ profession, sideWork, digest, recorded: false }` for onboarding to record after `venture.create`. Words in `app/ui/src/text/en/compliance.json`.
- **Fake engine**: `venture.compliance.get` is a before-vault method; both methods and the regulator refusals answer as the engine does.

## For the integrator (onboarding helper)

Mount `ComplianceCheck` right after the profession or family is picked. Pass the 6C2 family id or, once 6E6 lands, the profession id. Before the business exists, use `record={false}` and call `venture.compliance.set` with the returned answer after `venture.create`. No onboarding files were edited.
