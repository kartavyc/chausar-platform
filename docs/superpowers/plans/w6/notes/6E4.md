# 6E4: Document desk (D2)

## What was built

- **Shared parts pulled out of the tender desk** (the tender desk's behaviour and bytes are unchanged):
  - `playbooks/kit/documents/money.ts`: the amount extractor and the source-money receipts. `tender-desk/money.ts` is now a thin wrapper that keeps its names and the `tender_money_` prefix.
  - `playbooks/kit/documents/pack.ts`: Word, workbook and CSV pack parts. `tender-desk/export-core.ts` builds its response pack with these.
- **Playbook `document-desk`** (`playbooks/document-desk/`):
  - Offers: handbooks and SOPs, project reports (DPR or CMA), estimates, thesis editing, technical writing.
  - `core.ts` is pure, and the fake imports it too. It holds the document types and their starting outlines, and the figures table. Each line is quantity times unit amount, with BigInt only, rounded half up. The owner types the unit amount as a decimal and code converts it with `decimalMinor`. The table gives a total and a `document_figures_<digest>` receipt.
  - The money check refuses section text with an amount the receipt does not prove (`DOCUMENT_FIGURE_UNRECEIPTED`).
  - Two approvals, each bound to a digest. The brief (outline, files, figures) is approved before AI work. The finished document is approved before export.
  - The owner can take an accepted draft into the outline: any line that is an outline heading starts that section.
  - The export pack holds `document.docx` (with a Figures section and the receipt), `figures.xlsx`, `figures.csv` and `figures-receipt.json`. Both the engine and the fake use `storedZipBytes`, so the bytes match.
  - `desk.ts` holds one kind, `document`, one per order. Its actions are create, sources, outline, figures, section, approve-brief, take-draft, approve and export. Each one checks the digest. A client file that changed makes the document stale (`DERIVED_STALE`) until its files are chosen again.
  - Kit check `document-figures`: an AI draft may not write amounts of its own.
  - Rules: two house rules. Baseline: three cases, all passing.
  - Forget removes the client's documents (`privacy.ts`, wired in `network/forget.ts`).
- **AI work** (`agent-runtime/desk-context.ts`): a document desk run starts only from an approved brief (`DOCUMENT_BRIEF_REQUIRED`). The agent gets the type, title, outline and file ids, never figures to copy.
- **Shared files** (small additions only):
  - engine: `contracts.ts` (record kind `venture-document`, the type, error codes), `records.ts` (id prefix), `registrations.ts`, `definitions.ts`, `desks.ts`, `desk-types.ts`
  - UI: `types.ts` (`DeskPlaybook`), `fake-playbooks.ts`, `fake-kit-plans.ts`, `desks/index.ts`, `playbook-labels.json`
  - regenerated fixtures: `fixtures-kit-plan-picks.json`, `fixtures-start.json` (ways), `landed-kit-rules-data.json` (kept at indent 2)
  - test: the fake-plan test's id set.
- **UI**: `DocumentDeskDesk.tsx` (words in `pb-document.json`) and the fake `fake-document-desk.ts`, which uses the engine's own core. The browser metadata `document-metadata.ts` is generated from the definition.
