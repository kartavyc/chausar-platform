# Saved private investigations

`InvestigationService` saves an explicitly authored calculation plan and its exact evidence in the encrypted authority snapshot. It implements the bounded lifecycle behind `LIVE-D05-B04`, with resumption/context requirements `FIP-22-05/13/16/18` and the current-permission requirements of `FIP-08-03/08`. `FIP-22-11/12` additionally require the independently owned, qualified PostgreSQL search path; this module exports an authorized search projection and makes no full-text execution or recall claim.

The service stores `kind: private-investigation-v1` records in `State.answers`. Stable IDs, owner/member/permission epoch, branch, objective, revision, ordered step IDs, dictionary/version/definition hashes, explicit knowledge cut, and source/import scope are retained. The existing source deletion and retention traversal sees root `sourceIds`, `rowIds` and `receiptIds`, including excluded rows and supplementary source dependencies. This adds no separate persistence collection.

Creation requires an explicit ISO `asOf`, source-dependency IDs, ordered typed calculation requests, owner hypotheses, and caller budgets. Source IDs supplement the dependency and authorization scope; they do not filter the calculator. Every request inherits the same knowledge cut and branch. Creation pins the complete selected/excluded fact scope, relevant import bindings, source precedence and identity rules. Relevant changes reject reopening or pending execution with `INVESTIGATION_SCOPE_CHANGED`; they require an explicitly authored new investigation. Changed dictionary meaning likewise requires a new investigation.

Each advance executes only the first pending saved request. The calculator runs on the writable transaction draft, so its receipt and completed-step progress enter the outer authority commit together. A serialized Vault admits only one writer for an expected investigation revision. No chat text, caller replacement request, provider or action decides the next step. Pause/resume preserve completed receipt IDs; reopening, resumption, capsules, row drilldown and search projections recheck current authority, source scope, dictionary pins, semantic readiness, and every required actual receipt. Missing, stale or changed evidence is refused. Owner-authored hypotheses remain separate from measured figures and always carry `causalStatus: not-established-cause`, including an owner assessment of `supported`.

Public methods take the context and a separate investigation ID. `create` takes `InvestigationCreate`; `get` and `list` are reads. `advance`, `resume` and `cancel` take `{ expectedRevision }`; `pause` also requires `reason`. `revise` accepts `{ expectedRevision, reason, objective?, hypotheses?, budget? }`. The plan, knowledge cut, and dictionary binding cannot be amended in place. `capsule` returns a deterministic exact manifest with the whole saved plan, completed/pending obligations, actual receipt figures and definition hashes, coverage limits and owner hypotheses. Its completeness label means coverage of the persisted plan, not fulfilled business outcomes or a complete causal explanation.

`drilldown` takes `{ receiptId, offset, limit }` and returns a bounded page of current typed calculator receipt rows. Source descriptions and arbitrary fact fields are never projected. A page is labelled `bounded-receipt-page`; it is not labelled complete evidence. `searchDocuments` projects current validated private records with their IDs, revisions and content hashes. Root combines this projection with its separately validated decision journal before PostgreSQL search. Unavailable private records are excluded from the eligible projection; an empty or ranked search result cannot establish universal absence.

Budgets are explicit capacities, without business-cost defaults. Technical ceilings are 64 steps, 50,000 receipt-lineage rows, 1,048,576 context bytes, 64 owner hypotheses and 256 amendments. Context budgets must be at least 512 bytes. Receipt-row accounting includes completed receipt lineage and exclusions, plus a conservative selected/excluded-row estimate for the next request; duplicate references to one receipt are counted once, while different receipts each consume capacity. An overestimate can pause before a calculation. Plans above the step budget retain every unfinished obligation and pause. Exact context overflow pauses without sampling or inventing results; already measured progress remains retained. The owner can explicitly revise capacity and resume.

The unit fixtures use literal July collections `260000` and August collections `100000` minor units, real calculator/memory services, and a real encrypted experimental-file Vault for revision racing and reopen. They cover cut/resumed equivalence, excluded/empty source permissions, deletion/retention, plan/definition/scope/receipt drift, private ownership, source-tainted writer refusal and budget limits. They establish bounded local synthetic behavior. Production PostgreSQL durability/search, native arithmetic, independent recall qualification, GUI/route acceptance, provider turns and business outcomes require their own evidence. Full connector, native, hosted and enterprise scope remains open; vendor qualification and the blocked dedicated security audit are unchanged.
