# Reviewed personal lessons: isolated integration contract

This lane implements the staged lesson lifecycle specified in the live Questlog, Day 8 block 8 (`DAYS[7].blocks[7]`), and [the final plan, section 22.15](specs/CHAUSAR-FINAL-IN-DEPTH-PLAN.md#2215-lessons-and-reusable-guidance). Its exact baseline is `7bcb3b0cff25c1f872d8159049b234d5dd9f86f4`.

`platform/runtime/lesson-contracts.ts`, `lesson-evidence.ts` and `lessons.ts` are independent of Engine/RPC registration. Parent integration owns durable storage, installed evidence/target/evaluation authorities, the Friday review surface and the context consumer. The existing structural mapping learner and fact/identity memory remain separate.

## Authority and storage hooks

Construct `LessonService` with a `LessonStore` and explicit `LessonServiceOptions`. The store contains only a workspace ID, episodes and lesson records. It must serialize updates, pass a detached draft to the synchronous callback, and atomically commit on success or roll back on any exception. A successful returned decision records protocol state; it does not execute any business effect or write a preference configuration.

The service has no business `StateStore.update`, ontology, recipe, calculation or effect execution capability. Its write capability is confined to `LessonState`. Do not adapt the lesson store by giving callbacks an unrestricted mutable business `State`.

Every installed authority exposes a synchronous, read-only `authorityRevision()` token. It must change whenever that authority's values, permissions, schemas, qualification, provenance or available records change. The service checks a combined authority basis before and after each transaction and batch read. A changed basis rejects the operation before commit/release. Post-commit reads also check current authority; later revocation makes previously valid approval unusable.

- **Evidence:** `createLessonEvidenceAuthority(readCurrentState, resolver)` requires a supplied resolver and exposes `workspaceId()`, `authorityRevision()`, `authorize()`, `capture()` and `assertCurrent()`. It sends a frozen, detached current state, context and reference to the resolver. It enforces current membership/epoch, source/branch access, explicit clean taint classification, complete sorted source/branch pins, source markings and exact revisions. It detects authority changes during resolver callbacks. Full released snapshots, including summaries and source marks, must match the candidate's captured snapshots.
- **Personal targets:** supply `resolve()`, `validateChange()` and `authorityRevision()`. Only explicitly installed `personal-preference`, `reminder-timing` and `shortlist-order` targets are accepted. Resolve the original owner's current permission epoch, immutable target revision, digest and configured value. The target digest is `digest(targetWithoutDigest)` using the existing canonical digest. Validate the entire proposed after-value against the owner-supplied schema. No target schema, timing, ranking or action behavior is chosen here.
- **Evaluations:** supply `resolve()`, `assertSourceAccess()` and `authorityRevision()` over genuinely authorized, completed or explicitly unsuccessful evaluation records. This authority is optional for proposing candidates; it is mandatory before binding results or issuing human review. There is no fallback evaluator or generated score.

The evidence factory's authority token hashes the current business-state snapshot using the existing `hash()` JSON semantics, including normal omission of optional undefined fields. Its state reader and resolver must retain the installed authority, not synthesize a clean copy of external evidence. Target and evaluation tokens must include their complete access/configuration basis even if their records live outside that business snapshot.

## Existing evidence adapter requirements

References identify existing `job` or `calculation` records; the lesson service does not invent a job, calculation or source. The supplied resolver must preserve all original release guards and report unavailable evidence without releasing its content.

For jobs, retain the job's pinned owner ID/epoch/role, objective revision/digest, execution/run/plan/result bindings, complete source/branch lineage, verified settlements and the installed deliverable guard. Reject aggregate or per-settlement taint and stale/unaccepted/otherwise inadmissible results according to the real job authority.

For calculations, reuse the installed current-receipt authority, preserving private receipt ownership/epoch, receipt and fact retention, row and exclusion access, source tombstones, current definition/recipe/dictionary bindings and explicit freshness. `ConversationHistoryService.currentReceipt` is an existing pattern. A bare `CalculatorService.receipt` call or a list of source IDs alone does not establish all these guarantees. The receipt schema does not supply a universal taint verdict: classification must come from an actual supplied authority. Never infer clean taint from a number or from absence of a taint field.

The evidence pin's SHA-256 digest binds the resolver's complete immutable revision and all released source markings/summary. Branch/source identifiers are sorted, distinct and nonempty. Applicability uses explicitly named branches and the exact complete evidence source set; no wildcard applicability or permission broadening is inferred.

## Lifecycle and planned UI adapter

All candidate pins are `{ id, revision, digest }`. The digest covers the complete candidate: exact before/after change, target pin, evidence snapshots, source marks, applicability, counterexamples, owner, episode IDs and optional predecessor. Returned objects are detached.

1. `propose(context, proposal)` records separate immutable episode snapshots and a `candidate-lesson`. Supply title, rationale, existing evidence references, exact change, owner applicability and counterexamples. Before must match the configured target, or the explicitly superseded approved predecessor's after-value. Proposing grants no current guidance.
2. `revise(context, candidatePin, proposal)` appends an immutable revision. Earlier evaluations/review tokens do not authorize it. Approved/rejected/retired/superseded records require a fresh candidate instead of revision.
3. `bindEvaluations(context, candidatePin, { open, hidden })` resolves exact run references and binds both summaries. Missing, incomplete or declined evaluations and any paired-metric regression block review/promotion. Equal scores pass their supplied directions; no improvement threshold is invented. A blocked revision cannot silently replace its evaluation; submit an explicit revised candidate.
4. `review(context, candidatePin)` issues an evaluation-bound `LessonReview` only after passing current-authority checks. The reviewer must be the pinned personal owner with current approval permission, in the untainted desktop app. This is a protocol token for the parent-owned human screen, not an autonomous decision.
5. `decideLesson(context, { candidate, reviewId, reviewDigest, decision, reason })` accepts `approve`, `reject` or `defer`, rechecking the exact candidate, latest review, evaluations and current authority. Invocation context is detached and frozen, so queued caller mutation cannot clear taint or change the originating channel. Defer consumes the old decision path; rebinding results requires a new human review. Approval produces eligible personal guidance. Overlapping guidance requires explicit supersession; no priority/ranking is chosen. Supersession uses the exact prior scope and atomically retires its current status. Its predecessor must remain approved with current evidence/target/evaluation authority while the successor is evaluated, reviewed and decided.
6. `applicable(context, { branchId, sourceIds, conditions })` returns approved guidance only when its exact source set, named branch and declared conditions match and no counterexample matches. It rechecks the target, evidence, approval-owner epoch and bound evaluation provenance. The returned change contains only kind, target ID and the approved **after** value; the obsolete before value is kept out of current guidance.
7. `retire(context, candidatePin, reason)` removes approved guidance from current applicability. `resolveGuidance(context, pins, scope)` refuses retired/superseded/rejected/stale or out-of-scope references from old summaries. Store references in summaries and re-resolve them; never rehydrate historical prose as an instruction. `history(context, id)` preserves immutable versions/events only under current evidence, target and evaluation authority. A changed/revoked historical evidence or target pin can make history unavailable; history access is not a permission bypass.

For the planned frontend methods, `memory.lessons` can delegate to `lessons(context): LessonView[]` and `memory.decideLesson` to `decideLesson(context, LessonDecision): Promise<LessonView>`. A view contains `candidate` (evidence/source marks, applicability/counterexamples and exact diff), `status`, optional bound `evaluation`, and optional `review`. The parent-owned screen must obtain the current review token and display both results before submitting a decision. The RP-04 screen inventory proposes these names but supplies no finalized RPC payload; this document offers compatibility hooks, not registered endpoints. Friday scheduling and the screen remain parent-owned.

Episodes, candidate lessons and approved personal guidance are distinct. An approved lesson is still personal guidance: neither its approval nor its prose creates an approved company procedure or published business rule. Those need their separately governed owner/specialist processes. Protected/unknown change kinds, including dictionaries, recipes and published calculations, are refused before target resolution.

## Evaluation provenance and qualification limits

Each set records its exact run reference, evaluator authority ID, qualification, suite/harness/build/configuration pins, comparison ID/time, guidance-off baseline digest, exact guidance-on candidate pin, status, clean source provenance and paired finite metrics with supplied directions. Open and hidden must share the candidate, comparison, baseline, build, configuration and qualification. Their authorities, suites, harnesses and metric names may differ (Bench and Con have different responsibilities). `lessonEvaluationDigest(result)` hashes the whole result with only `reference.digest` omitted. No hidden question text enters this contract.

`synthetic-protocol` means synthetic lifecycle verification only. The test fixtures do not certify a real hidden suite, model route, business outcome, timing or ranking policy. `owner-qualified` is an explicit designation supplied by the installed evaluation owner; this service does not perform qualification or establish the quality of that authority. Product integration must supply actual Bench/Con results and their owner-approved qualification/admission process before claiming real evaluated lessons. No production evaluation authority was installed in this lane.

No nightly proposal job, cap/budget, model route, Friday scheduling, effect execution, context assembly, frontend, native or packaging changes are included. Those require configuration and the parent integration owners.
