# Venture launcher: the launch screens (P7)

This package is the renderer side of the venture launcher. A new person sees one question, "What service business do you want to start?", types one sentence and sees a plan at once, with nothing saved and no key asked for. After that it is one clear step at a time: keep the plan in an encrypted vault, accept it, hand the brief to a builder, then run the business from seven places. The twelve original views stay reachable, unchanged, under "Back office". The same files run in the desktop window and in the paired local browser.

The full description of the screens and the method table is the Launch section of [../frontend.md](../frontend.md).

## How the screens behave

- **Onboarding.** Idea, then the plan from templates (price and tax set inline; choosing per project or per hour asks `venture.preview` again with `pricingModel`, so the offer is worded for how it is charged), then Keep it safe (working name, shown as a placeholder so typing starts a fresh name, and passphrase, with the workspace folder named as the name is typed), then the recovery kit (which names the folder and saves it beside the code), then the setup calls in the spec's order, then the plan in the vault and Build. An open vault with no business resumes at the idea and skips the passphrase.
- **Your business.** At rest: the version and source ("from built-in templates"), the name, the one line, and the offers with their prices. "More about the plan" (who it's for, what each offer includes, what the business knows), "Steps", "You approve" and "Back office" open in place. Every line keeps its label, receipt totals that open the receipt sheet, "Set your price", tax invoice details for a registered owner in a tax invoice country, "Change plan", "Rewrite with AI" (opens Add your AI when no model serves the main tier), "Accept plan" with the exact digest. Every change quotes the priced offers again, because a new plan version has a new digest.
- **Build.** The opening words of the brief come first, then "Checks passed." and "Read it" (the sheet holds the whole brief, its size, "Copy brief", "Save brief" and "Write it again"; a brief that fails its check shows the reason and "Write it again" at rest), then "Open in Lovable", "Open in Replit" with "Replit receives the brief when its page opens.", "Opened before" folded, then GitHub: the state word at rest, and the last change, the brief marker, UI checks, the address and "What Chausar can't see" in "Details". When the brief and GitHub both answer from recordings, one line at the top says "AI answers and GitHub reads here come from a synthetic recording." instead of two.
- **Clients.** Clients and orders. Each order opens to "Draft a reply", "Mark delivered" and "Prepare invoice" (or "Open invoice"). The invoice sheet shows the engine's document text, its notes, the experimental notice, and "Issue invoice" behind a box that names the exact total.
- **Agents.** The AI in use, one folded rehearsal line ("Rehearsal: 3 of 3 passed", counted from the rows) with Rehearse (one practice client per `venture.simulation.run` request, practice cases made from reviews included; the fold opens while it runs), the next move ("Move to …" or the blocked reason) with the whole ladder in "Launch stages", up to three draft replies whose client name opens the draft, and "How agents work" for the AI tiers, the playbook and what agents can do today. A waiting draft shows "This is exactly what will be sent to {model}" and the payload, with "Approve and send" behind a box. In a demo with recordings it says "AI answers here come from a synthetic recording." above the payload and the draft, and "This is exactly what Chausar would send to {model}. In this demo a synthetic recording answers instead.", wherever the sheet is opened from (Clients or Agents). A practice draft shows "Practice draft. Not for the client." and has no Accept, Copy or Save. The sheet reads the engine's `practiceNow`, `canApprove`, `canAccept` and `acceptBlocked` (the `DeliveryRunView` flags): Accept shows only where the engine takes it, with the engine's own reason when it refuses, and a waiting draft the engine would no longer send has no Approve. A rejected or edited draft offers "Practise this": it finds the review's `signal-recorded` event (the owner's review before an earlier failed check) and calls `venture.simulation.fromSignal`, then says "Added to rehearsal on Agents: {title}."
- **Money.** A period choice from the engine's own list (All time, This month, Last month, This year) plus "Choose dates" for a from and to, sent as `{ period }` or `{ from, to }`; the heading is the chosen days. Then the three books figures with their receipts, the notes in "About these figures", the experimental notice, open invoices, and "Back office books".
- **Today.** The owner's day as the engine counts it, what awaits the owner (each row is its own button, from the engine's `awaitingYou`, so practice drafts drop off once the business approves each draft), the books figures for this month, and the checklist and recent events folded.
- **Dates.** One day basis: the engine's `today` and `timeZone` from the books summary. The day line, and the date fields for delivery and payment, use the business's time zone; before any summary named it, this computer's own day.
- **Connections.** Starter cards first (several planned modes read as one chip, "planned: handoff, read, write"; "Visit site" sits inside each card's fold), then every other tool in one fold, "All 20 tools", grouped as before. "Open" only for an available handoff: Lovable and Replit go to Build, Bolt and v0 copy the brief. "Visit site" opens a vendor page. A planned chip has no button.
- **Add your AI.** The recommended pair (Haiku for quick work, Sonnet for plans, one key, two `venture.models.connect` calls), then every preset cheapest first with one word of state and its reason. The consent text is shown word for word with an unticked "I agree" box that gates the button. A preset the engine marks `not-live-verified` shows its `verificationNote` ("Not yet checked with a live key.") right beside "ready", once, and the recommended pair says it too. GPT-6 Luna has a model id field prefilled `gpt-6-luna`; the CLI rows say "Uses your own sign-in on this computer"; "Keep using templates" connects the built-in templates.

## Hand-off note

**Done.** Everything in section 10 of the build spec: the owned files, the `app.js` edits, the styles, the script tag, the eleven tests (as 35 test cases), the fixtures and these docs. After review: the workspace folder is shown on Keep it safe and in the recovery kit, the draft reply sheet says when a synthetic recording answers, the browser opens a builder's tab inside the click, a refused copy offers Save brief, and every step and approval carries its own label.

**Choices made where the spec left room** (the integrator or the founder can reverse any):

- `renderSetup` also hands over to `launch.afterOpen` after a vault opens there, so "I already have a business in Chausar" lands on Today (or back at the idea when the vault has no business). This is one more edit than the list in 10.1; without `launch.js` it does exactly what it did before.
- The top bar's avatar letter comes from the workspace name with or without `launch.js` (it was a hard-coded "K").
- The back office footer line ("Every result comes from your local engine.") is not drawn on launch places, to keep their word count.
- "Keep using templates" shows the templates' consent text as a line and needs no box: nothing is sent anywhere. Every connection that can send words needs the box.
- Dark mode covers the onboarding screens, the seven places and the launch sheets. The back office keeps its light look, because its stylesheet has about 190 fixed light colours; a token sweep would bring it along. When the owner opens a back office view, the rail and top bar turn light too, so one screen never mixes the two.
- Date fields (delivered on, received on) start at today's date as the engine counts it (UTC), because the engine's books periods and invoice dates are UTC. Between midnight and 05:30 in India that is yesterday's date; the owner can change it.
- When a pending request runs past 100 ms the area shows the still outline and one line of words. Under 100 ms nothing changes.

**For the integrator.**

- **Contract change needed.** In `contracts.ts`, `ModelConnectionRecord` and `ModelCallRecord` both declare `kind` twice: `RecordBase` sets it to the record kind (`'venture-model-connection'`, `'venture-model-call'`), and the record adds `kind: ModelRouteKind`. TypeScript reduces each type to `never`, so no literal satisfies `ModelStatusView['connections'][number]` or `ModelCallRecord`. Rename the route field, for example `route: ModelRouteKind`, in both types and in the packages that read it. The renderer does not read that field. The renderer fixture types its two connections through `unknown` until then. **Applied in Phase C.** Both record types now carry the route as `routeKind` in `contracts.ts` (commit `b8752df`), and the models package, the engine and e2e tests and the renderer fixture read them through the contract types (commit `9faee82`).
- In a browser, Open in Lovable or Replit opens an empty tab inside the owner's click, before `venture.build.handoff`, and sends the link there once it passes the checks; a blocked tab shows "Your browser blocked the new tab. Allow pop-ups for Chausar and try again." and sends nothing. Links opened straight from a click (Visit site, Create a read-only key) still use a temporary link. The paired-browser path is covered by tests with a fake `window.open`; no real browser opened a builder site.
- In the built-in browser used for the walk, `navigator.clipboard.writeText` was refused ("Write permission denied"), so Bolt's Open showed "This window could not copy. Use Save instead." and did not mark the handoff opened, as designed. The card now shows "Save brief" beside that error. `main.cjs` denies every permission check, so copying may be refused in the Electron window too. "Save brief" and "Save invoice" always work. If copying matters on the desktop, `main.cjs` could allow the `clipboard-sanitized-write` permission for the trusted document only. **Applied in Phase C:** `main.cjs` now allows that one permission for the app window on the trusted document and refuses every other (commit `b8752df`, tested with mocks in `venture-shell.test.ts`; not yet seen in a real Electron window).
- `launch.js` keeps literal copies of `INDIA_STATES`, and `app.js` of `EXTERNAL_ORIGINS` and `EVIDENCE_METHODS`. Tests compare each with the contracts, so a contract change shows up as a failing renderer test.

- The engine has no `workspace.list`, so the folder name is the owner's only way back in. The launch screens now show it on Keep it safe and in the recovery kit. The old open form still starts at `main`; if that should start at the last folder this browser created, `renderSetup` would need to read it from per-viewer storage, which is outside the 10.1 edits.

**Not verified in this run.** The Electron window itself (no `node_modules`; the IPC path is covered by the real preload and contract in the tests). Opening Lovable, Replit or a vendor site in a real browser: the walk avoided opening outside sites, and the link opener is covered by tests. The pixel look was checked only partly, because the browser pane was hidden for most of the walk and drew only parts of the page; layout facts were checked through the page's own measurements instead.
