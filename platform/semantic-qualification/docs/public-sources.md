# Public source discovery and rights boundaries

Research timestamp: 2026-09-30 23:07 UTC. Research used read-only web and authorized GitHub tools. No sample ZIP, database backup, merchant export, taxpayer record or account was acquired. The JSON register contains metadata and links, not admitted qualification datasets.

## Evidence classes and admission

Keep reference/schema documentation, coherent business books, independent company/configuration/version/template families, and synthetic cases separate. A schema reference supports a scoped field interpretation. A coherent business bundle also needs compatible configuration, source transactions, filters, source cut and independent source reports. Independent qualification holds out entire company and template/configuration/source-family origins, including clones and derivatives.

All 16 candidate records remain `discovery-only`, without intake receipts or local source-row artifacts. UCI has an explicit dataset license located; other findings have narrower scope or unresolved components. Admission requires exact artifact/version, sensitive-data review and reviewed permissions for acquisition, retention, parsing, model disclosure, evaluation, reuse/derived templates, redistribution and future training. A code license is not blanket permission for company exports. Unknown downstream permissions follow derivatives.

The 1,500–3,000 bundle range is aspirational planning input. This research acquired zero coherent books and zero independent real-company families. Synthetic fixtures remain a separate evidence class; they cannot establish observed vendor behavior, prevalence or correctness for arbitrary companies.

## Source-profile guard requirements

[source-profile-requirements.json](../fixtures/source-profile-requirements.json) records 8 unqualified profiles and 14 assertion IDs linked to 15 official references. These are data-only requirements for the root compiler: unimplemented guards must abstain, missing evidence must abstain, contradictory version/configuration must invalidate. They do not claim vendor qualification. No source samples were acquired during this follow-up.

- **Razorpay:** `RZP-ORDER-PAID-IS-NOT-NET-RECEIPTS` separates order state from net receipts because [order paid persists after a refund](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/orders/fetch-with-id/?preferred-country=IN). `RZP-REFUND-FINAL-STATE-AND-IDENTITY` requires linked identity and distinguishes [processed, pending and failed refund states](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/refunds/fetch-with-id/?preferred-country=IN).
- **Shopify:** `SHOPIFY-ORDER-TOTAL-BASIS` distinguishes [totalPriceSet before returns from currentTotalPriceSet after returns](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/order), preserving tax and currency roles and avoiding refund subtraction twice. `SHOPIFY-REFUND-MONEY-MOVED` follows the [Refund warning](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/refund): require associated transaction identity, [SUCCESS status](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/enums/OrderTransactionStatus) and [REFUND kind](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/enums/OrderTransactionKind) before asserting money returned. Pages displayed 2026-07; latest remains mutable. `SHOPIFY-DEMO-DRAFT-PROVENANCE` preserves generated origin and draft state: [generated test orders](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/stores/generated-test-data) for stores created after 14 September 2023 are drafts. No completion/mutation is authorized by this reference.
- **AIS/Form 26AS:** `AIS-VALUE-STAGES` preserves reported, system-processed and taxpayer-accepted/source-confirmed stages. `AIS-26AS-OVERLAP` forbids blind addition and requires matched source identities, assessment year and aggregation level. The [official FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/ais-faq) documents TDS/TCS in both sources; avoiding duplicate canonical transactions is a conservative inference, not proof that every row overlaps. Raw JSON field names remain unverified.
- **Tally:** `TALLY-NATIVE-JSON-RELEASE` requires a demonstrated TallyPrime release of at least 7.0 for native JSON, following [release7 notes](https://help.tallysolutions.com/release-notes-tallyprime-7-0/). Older/unknown sources need separately qualified XML/export profiles. `TALLY-XML-MESSAGE-VERSION` prevents using [XML message VERSION](https://help.tallysolutions.com/xml-integration/) as the application release.
- **Zoho:** `ZOHO-COUNTRY-EDITION-FIELDS` requires organization country/edition: [Credit Notes docs](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/credit-notes/) label GST fields India-only, VAT treatment UK-only and CFDI fields Mexico-only while showing combined examples. These demonstrate the API schema; their coherence as company books is unestablished. `ZOHO-COHERENT-ORGANIZATION-BUNDLE` requires compatible configuration and source reports.
- **PhonePe Pulse:** `PULSE-RIGHTS-CHANNEL-TENSION` records separate delivery scopes. The [official repository license](https://github.com/PhonePe/pulse/blob/main/LICENSE) is CDLA-Permissive-2.0; the [website terms](https://www.phonepe.com/pulse/terms-and-conditions/) restrict commercial use, derivatives and external correlation. Neither source expressly resolves overlapping applicability. Acquisition, reuse, redistribution and training remain on hold pending exact-artifact/channel review. `PULSE-AGGREGATE-RELEASE-LINEAGE` follows the [README](https://github.com/PhonePe/pulse): aggregate/restated series cannot be treated as merchant books or independent additive releases.

The 56-group roster and every import/read/write status remain unqualified. Public reference assertions are evidence to design deterministic guards and adversarial cases, not permission to enable a vendor profile or announce real-company correctness.
